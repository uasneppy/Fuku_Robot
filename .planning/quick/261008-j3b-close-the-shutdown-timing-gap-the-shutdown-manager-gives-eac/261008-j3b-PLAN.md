<objective>
Close the shutdown timing gap recorded in STATE.md ([Cross-phase] concern) and in `.planning/debug/resolved/undo-claim-spent-and-undone-label.md` (candidate cause "environment: shutdown ordering").

Today `alita/utils/shutdown/graceful.go` gives every handler a hard-coded 10 s (`context.WithTimeout(ctx, 10*time.Second)` in the LIFO loop) inside a 60 s global budget. `StopStaffActions` waits up to `staffActionStopWait` (30 s) for staff action and undo runs to mark unfinished groups interrupted, write `FinalizeAction`/`FinalizeUndo` and deliver their final summary. After 10 s the manager logs "timeout, skipping", runs the remaining handlers including `closeDBConnections`, and exits 0, so a slow run loses its record write and summary. AGENTS.md promises the staff drain 30 s and the lockdown drain 5 s.

Fix: add a per-handler timeout option to the shutdown manager (default stays 10 s for everyone else). Register the staff and lockdown drains through two small modules helpers that give each drain its own wait plus a 1 s grace. Keep the LIFO order and the 60 s global budget. Give the production container a 75 s stop grace so Docker's default 10 s SIGKILL does not cut the budget short. Document the rule in AGENTS.md.

Purpose: a graceful restart never closes the database under a staff action or undo that is still finishing inside its promised 30 s, so audit records stay finalized and summaries are delivered.
Output: manager option + testtools runner, two drain registration helpers, main.go wiring, end-to-end and manager tests, docker-compose stop grace, AGENTS.md rule.
</objective>
