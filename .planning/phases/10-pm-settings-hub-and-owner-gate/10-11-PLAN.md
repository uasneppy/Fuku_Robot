## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

After this plan the owner-only default holds on every door that exists before the private list: the `/start settings_<chat id>` deep link refuses a gated admin like in-group `/settings` and every press already do (plan 10-05), and it answers every outcome that could reveal the bot's membership with one text. This plan was split out of plan 10-05 in revision 2 (scope_sanity: 10-05 stood at 0.85 of the smart-zone budget). It runs after plan 10-06 because both edit `fuku/modules/settings.go` and `AGENTS.md`, and it keeps the deep link's Owner flag that 10-06 adds.

<objective>
Put the owner gate of plan 10-05 on the deep link, replace its three-phrase error mapping with the 4xx class rule, prove that every door decides the same, and describe the gate on every door in AGENTS.md and the permissions reference (D-12, D-15, D-16, D-18; UI-SPEC R3; research R-07, R-08).

Purpose: MENU-11 (owner only by default through the deep link too) and MENU-13 (the deep link's refusals are short, never name the Staff Group and never reveal whether the bot is in a chat).
Output: the gated `settingsStartDeepLink`, its rewritten test table and same-answer test, `TestSettingsGateAllDoors`, AGENTS.md and the permissions reference.
</objective>
