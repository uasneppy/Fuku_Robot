## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

After this plan an owner who never ran `/admincache` still finds their groups in the private list after a deploy, the record corrects itself hourly, and `/settings` in a group repairs that group's record on the spot.

<objective>
Add the startup backfill and the hourly sweep of `chat_admin_index` as one background loop modelled on the Staff Group sweeper (D-05; research R-06): first cycle 30 to 90 s after start, then hourly, one replica per cycle through a Redis SETNX lock, paced Telegram calls, definitive facts only, a 429 backoff, and a context-aware stop wired into the shutdown order. Add the group `/settings` repair path (D-07; research R-05.4): after the choice message, the group's record is refreshed. Document the roster feed (plan 10-07), the sweep and the repair path in AGENTS.md.

Purpose: MENU-10 (the record is filled and kept fresh without anyone acting, and the two repair paths of D-07 both work).
Output: `admin_index_sweeper.go` and its tests, the `main.go` wiring, the repair call in `settings.go` with its test, AGENTS.md. The repair path moved here from the private-list plan during revision, so both repair paths and the sweep sit with the record's freshness rules.
</objective>

## Planner assumptions (flagged)

- Pace: 2 calls per group at 4 calls per second per runner, so 1000 groups take about 8 minutes, well inside the hour (research Open Question 3, resolved). A per-cycle cap is added only if the owner reports slow cycles.
- First run 30 to 90 s after start (assumption A5): slightly more load right after a start than the staff sweeper's 1 to 5 minutes, because an empty list after a deploy is visible to owners; the window is a variable.
- The repair refresh runs synchronously after the reply (research R-05.4: two extra Telegram calls on a rare command); only a sender the gate let in reaches it.
