## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

After this plan the Main page looks like the owner's GroupHelp reference: a two-column grid of page buttons with each page's state in a status block above, the owner's Access button on its own row, and the right way back on every page. This plan also closes the phase gate.

Purpose: MENU-15 (every page in a two-column grid with Close, in the opener's language; every page has Back), MENU-12 (Access is shown only to the owner, in its own row), PLAT-05 (the rendered labels fit every language).
Output: the renderer, `settings_main_test.go`, the rewritten tests, AGENTS.md and `10-SPIKE.md`.
</objective>
