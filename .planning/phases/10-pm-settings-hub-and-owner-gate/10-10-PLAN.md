## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

After this plan the Main page looks like the owner's GroupHelp reference: a two-column grid of page buttons with each page's state in a status block above, the owner's Access button on its own row, and the right way back on every page, including Groups from a private Main to the list. This plan also closes the phase gate.

</objective>
