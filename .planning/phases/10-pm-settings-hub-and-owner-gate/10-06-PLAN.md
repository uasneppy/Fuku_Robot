## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

After this plan the owner can decide: an Access page, reached from the Main page and shown only to the live owner, turns "Admins can change settings" on with two taps and off with one, and the change holds on every replica at the next press.

<objective>
Add the Access page (page key `a`, control `s`, page action `ay`) with its two-tap ON card and one-tap OFF, the owner-only page flag and right, and the one apply function for the switch (D-08, D-09, D-10, D-11, D-12, D-19; research R-07 "Owner right", Pattern 1).

Purpose: MENU-12 (the owner switch, owner only), MENU-14 (applies on every replica at the next press), MENU-11 (the Access controls are refused to everyone but the live owner).
Output: `settings_access.go`, its test file, the OwnerOnly and Owner additions in the page registry and press pipeline, the deep link's Owner flag and AGENTS.md.
</objective>
