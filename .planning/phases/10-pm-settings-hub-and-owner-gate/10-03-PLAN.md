## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

After this plan, the owner-only default is real: an admin who is not the owner cannot open or use the settings menu through any door that exists today, while the owner and (when the switch row is on) allowed admins work as in v1.0.

<objective>
Implement the owner gate (UI-SPEC step 4b) once, as `settingsGate`, with its owner-state read (`getChatAdministrators` scan, 3 s bound, per-chat singleflight, 30 s positive-only memo, the deleted-account heuristic) and the client-error classifier, and put it on the three doors that exist now: in-group `/settings` (with the anonymous re-entry), every button press, and the `/start settings_<chat id>` deep link, whose error mapping moves from three phrases to the 4xx class rule (D-13, D-15, D-16, D-17; research R-04, R-07, R-08).

Purpose: MENU-11 (owner only by default on every door), MENU-13 (short refusals that never name the Staff Group; commands unchanged), MENU-14 (the switch is read fresh at every press; the anonymous owner is the tapper).
Output: `settings_gate.go` plus tests, the three door changes, the deep link test rewrite, AGENTS.md and the permissions reference.
</objective>

## Planner assumptions (flagged, non-blocking)

- **A1 (deleted-account owner):** for a group whose owner deleted their account, `getChatAdministrators` is assumed to return a creator entry with first name "Deleted Account", no last name and no username. `settingsCreatorGone` treats that entry as no live owner, so admins are let in (D-15). Per the orchestrator, there is no human-verify checkpoint for this (the research suggested one); the live confirmation is a manual, non-blocking check listed in the phase SPIKE file (plan 10-08). If the real entry differs, the gate fails closed: admins of such a group get `settings_check_failed`, never wrongly let in.
- **A2:** a bot that is only a plain member is assumed able to call `getChatAdministrators`; if it is not, gated admins get `settings_check_failed` (fail closed). Manual, non-blocking check.
- **A4:** an anonymous creator's `getChatMember` still reports `creator`; otherwise the anonymous owner is gated like an admin (fail closed).
- **Gated Close (UI-SPEC unresolved):** every press is gated, Close included, as MENU-11 and "when unclear, refuse" require; a gated admin deletes the stale menu with Telegram's own delete. Accepted limit.

<assumption_delta_decision>
- Signal: the detector fired on "pluralization" ("a stale owner switch on another replica").
- Noun now primary: the live group owner (creator) is the primary settings authority; administrators become an owner-granted variant through the switch.
- Decision: promote for authority (the gate is the one function every door calls; admins are let in only through it), no-change for replicas (several replicas were already the v1.0 target, PLAT-01, and the switch is a fresh PostgreSQL read, so no replica-local identity is introduced).
- Invariant test: `TestSettingsGateAllDoors` here and `TestSettingsAccessAcrossReplicas` in plan 10-04 go red if a door or a replica stops honouring the owner-primary model.
</assumption_delta_decision>
