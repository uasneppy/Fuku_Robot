## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

This plan is the shared foundation every later slice of the phase stands on: the two tables, their repositories and the test harness (the research's Wave 0, orchestrator guidance 4). Its tracer proves the switch row end to end from the migration to a fresh read.

<objective>
Create the stored state of the phase: the owner switch (`chat_settings_access`, read fresh, D-12) and the bot's record of each group's owner and admins (`chat_admin_index`, a hint for the private list, D-02, D-06, D-15), each behind its own repository with SQLite tests and PostgreSQL guarantees, plus the settings test harness changes that keep every Phase 9 test green once the gate lands (R-09).

Purpose: every later plan (gate, Access page, feed, sweep, hub list) reads or writes these tables; the harness seeding is the single biggest planning risk named by the research.
Output: one migration, two models, two repository packages with tests, Makefile integrity targets, the harness helpers, and the AGENTS.md Data, Testing and locale-count updates.
</objective>
