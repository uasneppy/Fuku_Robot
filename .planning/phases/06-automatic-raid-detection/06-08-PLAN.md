<objective>
Wherever a lockdown is described to an admin, an automatic one says it was automatic and why. /lockdownstatus, /lockdown during an active lockdown and the hourly reminder show "locked automatically" and the trigger sentence (count, window, percent) rendered from the row's trigger columns in the reader's language, instead of "started by" the bot and an empty reason.

Task 1 is the status and already-active replies. Task 2 is the reminder and the manual regression.

Purpose: D-13 ("the reason names the trigger and its count"), D-21 (numbers rendered per language), ROADMAP Phase 6 criteria 1 and 2 as seen by admins.
Output: the automatic variants of the status, already-active and reminder texts, two locale keys, tests.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** have raids detected and locked down automatically, **so that** my group is protected when no admin is online.

**This slice:** an admin who checks on a locked group learns it was locked automatically and by which trigger.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- An automatic lockdown is one whose trigger_kind is join_surge or flood (`lockdownStartedAutomatically(ld)`); every other kind keeps today's text. The trigger sentence is plan 06-07's `lockdownTriggerSentence`, from the row's columns, never from reason.
- /lockdownstatus: the first line becomes `lockdown_status_active_auto` (since), the second the trigger sentence; the removed, ban-failed, declined, unconfirmed and permissions lines stay as they are. /lockdown: `lockdown_already_active_auto` (since) plus the trigger sentence. The reminder: the trigger sentence where `lockdownReasonLine` would go.
- Environment: golangci-lint cannot lint Go 1.26 here, so `go vet -tags testtools` and `gofmt -l` stand in for `make lint`. No PostgreSQL server is available; never start one.
