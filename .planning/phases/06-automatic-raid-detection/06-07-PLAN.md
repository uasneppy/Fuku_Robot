<objective>
The people around an automatic lockdown are told. The locked group gets one notice saying the bot locked it on its own, why, and how to lift it; and when the bot detects a raid it cannot lock, it says so once a day to the Staff Group and the log channel instead of failing silently.

Task 1 is the end-to-end notice. Task 2 is the once-a-day "can't lock" warning to both destinations.

Purpose: D-13 (public notice), D-14 and D-19 (warning), ROADMAP Phase 6 criteria 1 and 2 as seen by members and staff.
Output: raidPostNotice, raidWarnCannotLock, lockdownTriggerSentence, the moved Redis test helper, locale keys.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** have raids detected and locked down automatically, **so that** my group is protected when no admin is online.

**This slice:** members see why the group went quiet, and staff learn when the bot saw a raid it could not stop.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The notice is posted only for the Started outcome, by the raidFire call that won the latch and lockdown.Start, after the lock is confirmed, in the group's own language (`staffChatTranslator(chat.Id)`), through `lockdownPaced` under a 30-second bound (`raidPostTimeout`). It carries the trigger line, `lockdown_note_approved_muted`, and the delete and invite notes /lockdown adds when the bot lacks those rights (from `res.BotMember`), so members read what /lockdown would have said. A failed send is logged at warn and nothing else happens.
- The warning outcomes are NotSupergroup, BotCannotRestrict and LockFailed (a definitive refusal; research Pitfall 9). BotCheckFailed, PermissionsUnreadable, StateFailed and LockUnknown only log. One `SET NX EX 86400` on `alita:raid:{<chat>}:warn` gates both copies (D-19); a Redis error or a lost SetNX sends nothing. Destinations are resolved like the alert's: the Staff Group from `staff.GetLinkOfGroupFresh` (Staff Group language) and the log channel from `actionlog.Destination(chat, logchannels.CategoryAdmin)` (group language, starting with the header Destination returns). A lookup error skips that copy.
- `lockdownTriggerSentence(tr, kind, count, window, percent)` renders the time-less trigger sentence (`lockdown_trigger_join_surge`, `lockdown_trigger_flood`); the warning uses it now and plan 06-08 reuses it for /lockdownstatus. The group title is HTML-escaped and spliced after translation through `lockdownGroupToken`.
- The warning texts start with the warning marker and join `lockdownWarningKeys` (they report something the bot failed to do).
- `withMiniredis` moves from `antiraid_miniredis_test.go`, which held only that helper, to `testredis_test.go`, beside a `withMiniredisServer(t) *miniredis.Miniredis` that returns the server; `newLockdownEnv` keeps it on the env (`env.redis`) so a test can fast-forward Redis time (the 24-hour dedupe). This also unblocks deleting the antiraid tests in plan 06-10 (research Pitfall 1).
- Environment: golangci-lint cannot lint Go 1.26 here, so `go vet -tags testtools` and `gofmt -l` stand in for `make lint`. No PostgreSQL server is available; never start one.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Group titles to staff-facing HTML | The group title is set by its admins and reaches the Staff Group and the log channel |
| Raid traffic to outgoing messages | A raid repeats the trigger many times; each repetition must not post again |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-06-22 | Denial of Service | warning or notice spam during a sustained raid | medium | mitigate | the warning is gated by SET NX EX 86400 per group across replicas, the notice is posted only by the Started outcome, every send is paced fleet-wide (TestRaidCannotLockWarnsOncePerDay, TestRaidAutoNotice) |
| T-06-23 | Tampering | HTML injection through the group title | medium | mitigate | the title is escaped and spliced after translation through lockdownGroupToken; the trigger sentence holds only numbers |
| T-06-24 | Information Disclosure | a warning reaching a chat that is not the group's Staff Group or log channel | medium | mitigate | destinations come from the uncached staff link and actionlog.Destination with the admin category, as the alert's do |
| T-06-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | low | accept | No package installs; go.mod and go.sum are unchanged |
</threat_model>
