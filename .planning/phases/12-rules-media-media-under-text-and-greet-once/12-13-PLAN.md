## Planner assumptions and resolutions (flagged)

- UI-SPEC table row 3 ("unchanged") and check 8 ("a Rules refusal never carries the wording of `settings_edit_refuse_too_long_filled`") disagree for a no-media Rules text over 4096. Resolution (research Open Question 10, orchestrator note): with media Rules uses the new keys; with no media the existing key stays, and `TestSettingsEditCheckRules`' existing 4097 case keeps asserting it. The check-8 sentence is tested for the with-media refusals only.
- The media-side measure for Rules is heading plus the stored rules text normalized for HTML; when the text is empty the caption is the heading alone, and measuring `heading + ""` over-counts by the two newline units of the heading form, which is conservative (research S-03).
