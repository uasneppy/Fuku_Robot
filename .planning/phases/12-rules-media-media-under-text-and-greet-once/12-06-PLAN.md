## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** rules with media and buttons, media shown under its text, and members greeted only on their first join, **so that** my messages look and behave the way I set them.

After this plan the settings menu refuses a forged switch press (a control the page does not have, or a value that is not 0 or 1) before it asks Telegram anything, which the new Media under text and Greet once switches rely on.

<objective>
Add the control-level twin of `settingsPageAction.Validate` and a control existence check to step 1 of the press pipeline, and declare the 0-or-1 rule on the editor's existing switches (UI-SPEC check 3; research S-05).

Purpose: plans 12-10, 12-11 and 12-12 add the controls `o` (Welcome only) and `u` (Welcome, Goodbye, Rules); `t=o` on Goodbye or Rules, and `t=e` or `t=d` on Rules, must be invalid requests before any I/O.
Output: one field, one helper, two Validate declarations, one test file.
</objective>
