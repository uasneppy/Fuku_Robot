## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** rules with media and buttons, media shown under its text, and members greeted only on their first join, **so that** my messages look and behave the way I set them.

After this plan the database layer can claim a member's first welcome exactly once across replicas, store each new switch and rules part on its own, read a rules row whole without creating it, and tell the group's language by its chat ID.

<objective>
Add the greet-once repository, the column-targeted writers, the whole-row rules loader and the group language accessor, each with its own tests (D-04, D-11, D-13, D-15, D-17; research S-02, S-03, S-04; UI-SPEC M3).

Purpose: 12-09 claims through `ClaimGreeting`; 12-08, 12-10, 12-11 and 12-12 write through the new writers; 12-12 and 12-14 read through `LoadRulesSettingsContext` and `GetGroupLanguage`.
Output: one new repository file and its tests, writers and tests in three packages.
</objective>
