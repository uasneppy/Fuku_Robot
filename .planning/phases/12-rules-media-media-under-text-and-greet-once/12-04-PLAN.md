## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** rules with media and buttons, media shown under its text, and members greeted only on their first join, **so that** my messages look and behave the way I set them.

After this plan a GIF the owner sets as a welcome or goodbye is a real animation that can carry its caption above it, and the sender knows how to put a caption above a photo, video or GIF without changing any message saved before.

<objective>
Add the animation type and its send, split the greeting media extraction from the notes and filters one, and teach the sender the caption-above flag through one eligibility predicate (D-06, D-07, D-08; research S-01, Pitfalls 1 and 2).

Purpose: Media under text (plans 12-08, 12-11, 12-12, 12-14) needs a GIF that Telegram can place a caption above; documents cannot.
Output: the constant, the extraction split, the sender additions, their tests, the editor's GIF word, and the tests that encoded "a GIF is a document" flipped on purpose.
</objective>
