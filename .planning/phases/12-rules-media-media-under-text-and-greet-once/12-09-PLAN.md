- A 429 counts as a definite refusal (research Open Question 5: Telegram answered "too many requests" without processing, assumption A4). - Accepted limits recorded in AGENTS.md (research S-02): a basic group's migration to a supergroup changes the chat ID and its members are welcomed once more; a welcome switched off records nobody, so members who joined meanwhile are welcomed once when it is back on; bots that join are claimed like any member.
