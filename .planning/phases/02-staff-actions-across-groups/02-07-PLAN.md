<objective>
One summary that never lies by omission. While groups finish, the Staff Group sees the card fill in, edited at most every 2.5 seconds, with a tally and lines that never move. At the end every linked group is done, skipped or failed. A summary too long for one message collapses only the done lines and sends the overflow as continuation messages. The final result survives rate limiting, a deleted card and a bot shutdown. Task 1 builds the renderer test-first. Task 2 builds the progress coordinator, final delivery with retries and fallback, the shutdown drain in main.go, and the end-of-phase live check.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 ("one summary message that updates as groups finish ... No group is ever silently dropped"), STAFF-08, D-16 to D-18.
Output: the tally, collapse and continuation renderer, the batched coordinator, `StopStaffActions`, the main.go registration, the locale keys and the AGENTS.md rule.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** the issuer watches one message fill in, and every group is accounted for even under rate limits, huge group counts or a restart.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions and Discretion)

- OQ4 (crash mid-run): a graceful shutdown finalizes every unfinished line as "failed: interrupted by restart" and delivers the summary. A hard crash (SIGKILL, OOM) can leave ⏳ lines on the card. That is accepted for Phase 2, because re-running the same command is safe: the decision table never shortens or lifts anything. Phase 3's durable audit record could back a resume.
- A1: Telegram publishes no edit rate. The 2.5 s batching (D-16) sits inside the documented ~1 message per second per chat guidance, and a 429 on the final edit is retried after retry_after.
- Card deleted or not editable (Discretion): the final summary goes out as a new message through `sendStaffNotice`. Progress edits that fail are logged and skipped; only the final delivery falls back.
- Continuation messages appear only in the pathological case where skipped and failed lines alone exceed one message. STAFF-08 ("no group silently dropped") outranks D-09's "one message" there.
- After StopStaffActions has run, a late Confirm starts a run whose context is already cancelled. Every group then reads "failed: interrupted by restart", so no extra refusal text is needed.
