<objective>
Close verification Gap 1 (code review WR-01). Today the final summary runs on one shared 15 s context while a single retry_after wait may last 60 s, so under flood control the final edit gives up early and the fallback and continuation parts inherit an expired context and never retry: the bans land, but the issuer's card keeps showing the last progress snapshot. The progress ticker also keeps editing the card every 2.5 s through a 429, which feeds the flood.

Task 1 (tracer) gives every delivered message its own deadline sized to the retry cap and proves it at production scale with a deadline-checking virtual clock. Task 2 makes the progress ticker back off after a 429, has the final edit wait out that hold, clamps absurd retry_after values, and updates AGENTS.md.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 2 success criterion 4 ("waits and retries when Telegram says to ... No group is ever silently dropped"), STAFF-08, STAFF-12, D-16.
Output: per-message delivery budgets, the progress backoff, five new tests, the fake's request timestamps, and the AGENTS.md wording.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** the issuer always sees the final result, even when Telegram rate limits the Staff Group for longer than 15 seconds.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: fallback (the "post the summary as a new message when the card cannot be edited" path). Decision: no-change. Rationale: the detector matched the word "fallback" in plan 02-07's title; there is still exactly one card, one fallback path and one continuation sequence per run, and this plan only gives each of those existing messages its own deadline.
</assumption_delta_decision>

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Per-message budgets, not one larger shared budget: the verification gap allows either, and only per-message contexts stop a long edit wait from starving the fallback and the continuation parts. Each message may wait once at the cap before each of its 3 attempts, plus each attempt's own 10 s edit timeout, so the budget is 3 x (60 s + 10 s) = 210 s. Reversible: one constant.
- The progress hold uses the full retry_after (clamped to 1 h only to avoid overflow), not the 60 s cap, because skipping ticks costs nothing; the final edit's pre-wait for that hold is capped at 60 s like every other wait.
- On a 429 only, the progress snapshot is re-marked changed so the newest state is sent after the hold. Other progress-edit errors (for example a deleted card) keep today's behaviour of not re-marking, so a deleted card is not hammered every tick.
- StopStaffActions keeps its 30 s bound. A delivery that is still waiting out a 429 at shutdown is cut off with the process; AGENTS.md says so instead of promising delivery.
