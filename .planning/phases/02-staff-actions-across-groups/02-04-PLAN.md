<objective>
Name the target the way staff actually do. A Staff Group member can now name the target by `@username` (case-insensitive, never guessed), by a text mention, or by numeric ID. The issuer is told plainly when the bot has never seen a username, when a username is ambiguous, when they only replied to a message, or when they used a variant (`/sban`, `/dmute` and the others) that has no meaning in a Staff Group. Task 1 adds the repository lookup test-first. Task 2 wires parsing, resolution, hints and the refused variants, and completes the STAFF-13 and STAFF-06 regression suites for all 13 command names.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1 (`@username` or numeric ID, unseen usernames refused) and D-02, D-03, D-04.
Output: `user.FindUsersByUsername`, target forms in the staff parser, the refused variants, and the hint texts in 7 locales.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** target a bad actor by `@username` or mention, with ambiguity and unknown names refused, never guessed.

## Assumptions recorded (Discretion)

- Hint wording (Discretion): the texts below are proposals in plain language. All of them live in the 7 locale files.
- Username format: `^[A-Za-z0-9_]{4,32}$` after the leading `@`. Telegram allows 4-character collectible usernames, and a lookup that finds nothing is harmless.
- Ambiguous matches are listed up to `staffUsernameMatchLimit = 10`, newest last activity first. More than 10 users sharing one username is not realistic for this deployment.
- `LOWER(username) = LOWER(?)` scans the plain username index. That is acceptable at this deployment size, and no migration is added (RESEARCH Q6). The functional index stays optional.
- The anonymous check (plan 02-01) runs before the variant refusal, so an anonymous `/sban` in a Staff Group also gets "post as yourself".
