Task 2 wires the four commands, the four Telegram calls, the header and the summary reasons, extends the STAFF-13 and STAFF-06 regression suites, and adds the never-lift-a-ban rule to AGENTS.md.

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** `/mute`, `/kick`, `/unban` and `/unmute` from the Staff Group, each group decided from the target's live status, never lifting a ban by accident.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions and Discretion)

- OQ2, wording for a kicked (banned) target: D-11 is locked. It says a kicked target "counts as not in the group" and that `/mute` and `/kick` read "skipped: not in group". This plan uses that line for mute, kick and unmute on a kicked target. Research and the Discretion list suggested "skipped: already banned" for a mute; D-11's locked wording wins. The behaviour is identical (no call), the choice is reversible, and it is flagged for the owner at verification.
- OQ1 / A6: a staff `/kick` on a muted member applies, and the kick drops the mute, as today's per-group `/kick` does. D-12 ("never shortens an existing ban or mute") is read as covering actions that have an end date (ban, mute); a kick has none. Reversible (one table row); flagged for the owner at verification.
- Cross-action rules (Discretion), confirmed against RESEARCH Q1: a ban on a muted user applies (stricter). A mute on a banned user is skipped (no call). An unmute on a banned user is skipped (no call). A restricted non-member who is muted can be unmuted: the default permissions are written without making them a member.
- OQ6: the live check of the research flag (tdlib's final status-replacement step is inferred, A3) is a deferred human check in Task 2, not a gate. The design never sends restrict or unban(false) to a banned target, so it is safe either way.
- `/unmute` gives back the group's default permissions from a live `getChat`, and a `getChat` failure fails the group, exactly as per-group `/unmute` does (D-14).
