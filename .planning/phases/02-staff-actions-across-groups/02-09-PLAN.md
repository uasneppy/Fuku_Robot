<objective>
Close the pacer half of verification Gap 2 (code review WR-02). Today TelegramPacer.Do records Telegram's full retry_after as the shared block, and every other paced call on every replica then reserves a slot after that block and sleeps through it with no cap. A retry_after of thousands of seconds stalls every worker and every staff run fleet-wide, and a run can outlive its 30-minute target lock.

Task 1 (tracer) makes a paced call refuse a slot more than MaxWait away, without calling Telegram and without taking the slot, and proves it end to end: a staff run under such a block ends fast with every group "rate limited", and the next run works once the block is gone. ## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** a huge Telegram retry_after makes staff actions fail fast and visibly as "rate limited" instead of silently freezing every run across the fleet.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Primary noun: pacer block (alita:staff:pace:block). Decision: no-change. Rationale: there is still one fleet-wide pacer with one next-slot key and one block key; this plan only changes how a caller reacts to a slot beyond its cap. The orchestrator's "fallback" signal refers to plan 02-07's summary fallback, which this plan does not touch.
</assumption_delta_decision>

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The shared block is NOT clamped to MaxWait. The verification gap lists the clamp as optional. Research Q2 and plan 02-06 (threat T-02-27, TestTelegramPacerSharedBlock) chose to record Telegram's full retry_after so no replica sends into a flood Telegram asked it to wait out. With `wait` now refusing anything beyond MaxWait, the full block can no longer stall anyone: callers fail fast as "rate limited" until it ends. Clamping would also break TestTelegramPacerSharedBlock, which expects the block to outlast the reporting pacer's own cap. Reversible: one line in Do.
- A refused reservation takes no slot: the Lua script returns before its SET. Otherwise every refused caller would push alita:staff:pace:next one interval further and keep refusing callers after the block ended.
- retry_after is clamped to 86400 s (one day) before multiplication. Telegram never sends more; the clamp only stops an overflow into a negative or garbage duration.
