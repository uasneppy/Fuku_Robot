<objective>
Make the card trustworthy over time and across replicas. A card that nobody confirms expires visibly after 5 minutes. Concurrent or duplicate Confirm taps, on one replica or several, start exactly one run. A Confirm whose linked groups changed since the card was shown is aborted rather than acted on. Two staff actions on the same person never run at once. Task 1 adds the expiry timer and proves exactly-once Confirm. Task 2 adds the links-changed abort and the per-target lock, documents the staff actions in the `/staff` help text, and records the new keys in AGENTS.md.

Purpose: D-07, ROADMAP criterion 1 ("nobody else can confirm"), PLAT-01 for the card, and the Discretion item on concurrent staff actions.
Output: `scheduleStaffActionExpiry`, `expireStaffActionCard`, `staffLinksSignature`, the target lock helpers, the help text in 7 locales, the regenerated docs page and the AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** a card that cannot fire twice, cannot fire late, and cannot act on a group list the issuer did not see.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions and Discretion)

- A8, confirmed: abort at Confirm when the linked groups changed since the card was shown. A signature over the sorted group chat IDs is compared, not only the count, so swapping one group for another is caught too. Rationale: the card told the issuer "Applies to N linked groups" (D-06), and acting on a different set would act on something they did not confirm.
- A9, confirmed: a per-target Redis lock for the duration of a fan-out. Rationale: it closes the restrict-replaces-ban race between two concurrent staff actions on one person (RESEARCH Pitfall 11, research flag), which convergence alone (D-12, D-13) cannot close. A local admin racing within milliseconds remains an accepted tiny window.
- Optional per-issuer cooldown: dropped. The Confirm tap, the one-run-per-target lock and the shared pacer of plan 02-06 already bound cost, and a cooldown would slow staff during a raid.
- A restart loses the in-process expiry timer. The lazy path (any tap after expires_at) still expires the card, and the hash TTL removes it. Accepted.
- The help text keeps the existing `staff_help_msg` markdown style and uses no angle brackets, which would break HTML parse mode. Placeholders are written as plain words, for example "user".

## Trust Boundaries
