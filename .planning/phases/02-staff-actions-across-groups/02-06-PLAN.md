<objective>
Fan-out at scale, within Telegram's limits. Every Telegram call of a staff action goes through one pacer whose budget and `retry_after` block live in Redis, so several replicas share one rate. A 429 is waited out and retried, never mistaken for a failure, and never silently dropped. Groups run four at a time, a failure carries a plain reason from a live probe of the bot's rights, and a panicking worker still reports its group. Task 1 builds the pacer test-first. Task 2 wires it into the fan-out (including the Phase 1 owner recheck, through a seam that leaves its other callers untouched) and adds bounded workers and D-19 failure classification.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 4 ("stays within Telegram's rate limits even with many groups and several bot replicas, and waits and retries when Telegram says to"), STAFF-12, PLAT-01 and D-19.
Output: `ratelimit.TelegramPacer`, the paced fan-out with four workers, failure classification, the locale keys and the AGENTS.md rule.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** the action reaches every linked group, even many of them across several replicas, with every failure explained.

## Assumptions recorded (research)

- A5, pacer defaults: 100 ms fleet-wide interval (about 10 calls per second), 4 workers, 3 retries, and a 60 s cap on a single wait. They are tunable constants. Too aggressive shows up as retries, too slow as a longer run.
- A10: Telegram's published flood figures (about 1 message per second per chat, 20 per minute per group, about 30 per second in bulk) come from a summary of the official FAQ. The design depends only on the 429 plus retry_after contract, which is verified in the server and gotgbot sources.
- Summary edits are not paced through the shared budget: one coordinator per card owns them (plan 02-07) and handles its own 429s, as RESEARCH Q3 recommends.
- A `getChatAdministrators` that hits a 429 during the owner recheck is retried through the pacer. It never removes a link: only OwnerMismatch does (Phase 1 rule).
