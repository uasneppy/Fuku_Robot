## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** `/ban <ID> [reason]` from the Staff Group, Confirm, live per-group checks, one summary message.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions and Discretion)

- OQ3 / A2: staff actions need Redis (the card and, from plan 02-06, the shared pacer). Without a Redis client the command is refused with `staff_act_redis_unavailable` (fail closed). Reversible: a later degraded mode only adds a branch.
