<objective>
Timed actions. `/ban <ID> 2d spamming`, `/mute <ID> 30m`, `/tban` and `/tmute` from the Staff Group. The duration grammar comes from the same m/h/d/w rules as the per-group commands, through a new pure exported parser. Anything over 366 days is shown and applied as permanent. The end date is fixed once at Confirm, and the decision table's never-shorten and upgrade rules (D-12, D-13) now see real end dates. Task 1 writes the parsers test-first. Task 2 carries the duration through the card, Confirm, the header and the regression suites.

Purpose: ROADMAP criterion 1 ("Ban and mute can be timed") and D-01.
Output: `extraction.ParseDurationToken`, duration modes in the staff parser, `/tban` and `/tmute` in the Staff Group, duration labels in 7 locales.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** ban, mute or kick someone in every linked group at once, **so that** one command protects them all.

**This slice:** timed bans and mutes, with the duration visible on the card before Confirm.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions and Discretion)

- OQ5 (Discretion "Durations longer than Telegram's 366-day limit are treated as permanent"): only the staff parser converts an over-limit duration to permanent, and the card says "permanent (longer than 366 days)". Per-group `/tban` keeps refusing it with its existing reply (STAFF-13). Telegram never receives a raw clamp (Pitfall 10).
- D-01 "matches the existing m/h/d/w form" is read as exactly `^[0-9]+[mhdw]$`, lowercase, as the existing parser requires. "2D" is not a duration, so it lands in the reason, and the card shows "permanent" so the misparse is visible.
- The smallest token, `1m`, is 60 s, so the 30 s minimum of TemporaryUntilDate can never be hit.
- The label is "{n} minute(s)" and similar in each locale. True plural rules across 7 languages are out of scope for a confirmation label.
