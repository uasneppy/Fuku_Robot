## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The guide is hand-written and carries `<!-- MANUALLY MAINTAINED: do not regenerate -->` in its first 512 bytes, after front matter `title: Web captcha (Turnstile)` and a one-line description, in the style of `self-hosting/webhooks.md`; the directory's `meta.ts` lists no pages, so nothing else is registered.
- Proxy examples (Caddy and nginx) forward only the exact path `/captcha` and the prefix `/captcha/` to the bot's `HTTP_PORT`, terminate TLS, pass `X-Forwarded-For`, and show the matching `CAPTCHA_WEB_TRUSTED_PROXIES` value; a sentence says what goes wrong when the whole port is forwarded (metrics, pprof, webhook exposure) and that the bot warns at startup when `METRICS_AUTH_TOKEN` is empty.
- Troubleshooting: an endless Try again with widget codes 110100, 110110, 110200, 400020, 400021 or 400070 means a wrong site key or an unauthorized hostname (the page cannot tell, research Pitfall 19); a blank sheet only in Telegram Web points at frame-ancestors; `retry` versus `failed` (only a provably invalid or reused token, or one minted for another challenge, fails a member; anything else is retry); `/captchamode turnstile` refusals and what each needs; health 503 (not ready yet, or Redis down); members got math instead (the web captcha was unavailable at join); a hostname mismatch logged as a configuration fault.
- 08-SPIKE.md follows `07-SPIKE.md`'s skeleton (Why this exists, Before you start, What to run in order, What to read in the output, Decisions, Which constants to change afterwards, Checks only a live run can do, What the checklist never does) with a checklist in place of a tool run.
