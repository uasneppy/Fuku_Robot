## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** verify new members with Turnstile in a Telegram Mini App, **so that** bots stay out with little friction.

**This slice:** an admin types `/captchamode turnstile` and either gets the Turnstile check for every new member, or a plain reason why not and nothing changed.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Order of the refusals, all after `captchaAdminGate(bot, ctx, false)` and after the mode word is recognised: (1) no runtime installed (`captchaWebCurrent` nil, so the four settings were not set or the wiring of plan 08-10 is not in place) is `captcha_web_refuse_not_configured`; (2) the runtime's Redis does not answer a fresh PING within 250 ms (not the 5 s memo: the admin deserves a live answer) is `captcha_web_refuse_no_redis`; (3) `chat_status.FetchBotMember` (live, 8 s bound) not `BotMemberFound`, or a status other than creator or administrator with `CanRestrictMembers`, is `captcha_web_refuse_no_rights` (the pattern of `lockdown_start.go` lines 94-97). A bot username missing is treated as not configured.
- The rights named are only Restrict members: the math and text captcha need the same right to mute and unmute; deleting the challenge and held messages is best effort there too (the executor confirms against `SendCaptcha` and `handlePendingCaptchaMessage`; if the existing modes need more, the key text names it).
- Keys (UI-SPEC "Telegram-side Pieces", limits from its table): `captcha_mode_turnstile_desc` (en "Cloudflare Turnstile web check", at most 40 EN and 52 other), `captcha_web_refuse_not_configured`, `captcha_web_refuse_no_redis`, `captcha_web_refuse_no_rights` (the English from the UI-SPEC table, at most 160 EN and 224 other, HTML parse mode like the other captcha replies but holding no markup), all 7 files. Edited in all 7: `captcha_mode_specify` and `captcha_mode_invalid` list `<code>math</code>`, `<code>text</code>` and `<code>turnstile</code>`; `captcha_help_msg` adds one short line "Turnstile: a Cloudflare check on a web page" under the captcha types and `/captchamode <math/text/turnstile>`; `captcha_invalid_mode_error` says `'math', 'text' or 'turnstile'`. The mode words stay untranslated in every language. `captcha_help_msg` is 799 to 1003 characters today, far below Telegram's 4096, so one short line is safe.
- `docs/src/content/docs/commands/captcha/index.md` carries the MANUALLY MAINTAINED marker, so it is edited by hand (the research thought it was generated); the edit mirrors the English help: the Turnstile type, the `/captchamode <math/text/turnstile>` line, and one sentence that it needs the bot owner's web captcha setup (link to the self-hosting page plan 08-11 writes, `/self-hosting/captcha-web/`, the absolute style the other pages use).
