## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** the bot to also tell a punished person in private, from settings I set group by group in the bot's private
chat, **so that** they know what happened and why, while the punishment and the group message are never delayed or changed.

After this plan the admins can see in the log channel which private copies did not arrive.

<objective>
The log line (PMSG-07, D-21, D-24): one paced post in the group's log channel for an undelivered copy, gated by the group's option and the log
channel's admin category, and the hub's second option toggle.

Purpose: PMSG-07 as an engine feature every event with a private copy gets.
Output: one new production file, engine and page changes, strings, tests, AGENTS.md.
</objective>

## Planner assumptions and decisions (flagged)

- **When.** `runEventPM` calls `pmCopyPostLog` after `pmCopyAddDetails`, for the same condition (a final outcome other than delivered of a job that
  tried to send) and only when `o.Options.PMLog`. The two are independent: either, both or neither may be on.
- **The post (D-21).** `pmCopyPostLog` resolves `actionlog.Destination(&o.Chat, logchannels.CategoryAdmin)` (no channel or the category off posts
  nothing) and sends one message through `pmCopyPaced` with `SendMessageWithContext`, HTML, link previews off, like `sendStaffLogPost`: the header,
  then `#PM_COPY` on its own line, then `eventmsg_pm_log` in the group's language with `{user}` = `formatting.MentionHtml(id, name)` followed by the ID
  in a code tag, `{event}` = `eventPMEventName`, `{why}` = `eventmsg_pm_why_blocked` for blocked and `eventmsg_pm_why_failed` for every other outcome.
  A failed post is one Warn line with the chat ID and the Telegram code. The destination read is the job's only database read; after the database is
  closed at shutdown it fails and nothing is posted (D-13).
- **English drafts:** `eventmsg_pm_log` `📩 The private copy of the {event} message did not reach {user}: {why}.`; blocked `they have not started a
  chat with me, or they blocked me`; failed `Telegram did not deliver it`; line `Log line: {state}`; toggle `{state} Log line`; hint `Log line: a
  private copy that does not arrive leaves one line in the group's log channel, under its admin category.`
