## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** the bot to also tell a punished person in private, from settings I set group by group in the bot's private
chat, **so that** they know what happened and why, while the punishment and the group message are never delayed or changed.

After this plan the owner sets and switches on a private copy from the Messages page; the send path of plans 21-02 and 21-03 sends it.

<objective>
The private copy on the Messages page (D-22, D-23, D-25, D-26, D-28): one more message editor definition per event for the `pm` destination,
reached from the event screen through the new field `d`, with Set, Remove, Cancel and the typed reply through the editor and the typed-reply
door unchanged, a switch `hp` written through `applyEventPM`, the event screen's private copy line and row, the part apply functions taking a
destination, the strings, and the drift and authority tests that stand in for a parity test.

Purpose: the owner can now configure PMSG-01 for any event with a private copy spec; previews and the hub's states are plan 21-05.
Output: page and apply changes, six new strings and two updated, the PM tests, expected-set edits of the Phase 20 page tests, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
