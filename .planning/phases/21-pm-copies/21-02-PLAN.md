chat, **so that** they know what happened and why, while the punishment and the group message are never delayed or changed.

After this plan a private copy stored and switched on for a group is sent to the punished member after the next manual punishment there.

## Planner assumptions and decisions (flagged)

- **The group step is today's code.** `replyEventGroup` is the body of Phase 20's `replyEvent` after the read, returning the message it sent: the
  default and the refusal fallback go through `sendSuccess` (the same `c.Msg.Reply` and `scheduleBotMessage` as `replySuccess`, which now wraps it),
  the custom message through `media.SendGreetingMessage` exactly as before. `TestLegacyModerationRequests` proves the bytes; nothing of the group
  path is reordered.
- **The offer (D-11).** After the group step, whatever it returned, `offerReplyPM` looks up the event's spec; with a spec and a `Resolve(event,
  DestPM)` whose `Silent` is false it composes the copy with the moderation context's translator (the group's language), the actor of the group
  step (`actorIsAnonymous`) and the target, and calls `offerEventPM`. A failed read (`replyEvent`'s first branch) offers nothing. `replyEvent` still
  returns the group step's error.
- **The job (D-13).** `offerEventPM` takes a slot with `pmCopyJobs.startBelow(pmCopyMaxInFlight)` (a full pool sheds with one Debug line naming the
  chat, the user and the event; plan 21-03 tests it) and starts a goroutine that begins with `defer pmCopyJobs.finish()` and `defer
  error_handling.RecoverFromPanic(...)`, makes a context of `pmCopyJobTimeout` from `context.Background()` and runs `runEventPM`. In this plan
  `runEventPM` sends the composed message to the target's private chat through `pmCopyPaced` with `media.SendGreetingMessage` (no reply target, no
  topic) and logs the outcome at Debug with the chat ID, the user ID and the event (classification and fallback are plan 21-03). The job builds no
  `ReplyParameters` and calls no method named `Reply` (D-31).
- **The composer (D-12).** With a stored text, `renderEventText` fills it (Welcome placeholders for the target and the group, the event placeholders
  from `eventPlaceholderValues`, the `{rules}` button), and `r.PMNote`, when set, follows on a new line. With none, the text is
  `eventPMDefaultText`: the spec's `DefaultKey` through the translator with `{group}` the group's title escaped (and cut to 64 runes like
  `staffDisplayTitle`) and `{until}` (`formatChatTime` in `chatClockOf(chat.Id)`, `02.01.2006 15:04`) for a timed event, then on new lines
  `eventmsg_pm_reason` with `{reason}` escaped when there is a reason and the note when set. The keyboard is `keyboard.BuildKeyboard` of the stored
  buttons (with the `{rules}` button when the text added one), nil when empty; the media is the row's file and type (`db.TEXT` with no file).
- **The specs.** ban `eventmsg_pm_ban`, tban `eventmsg_pm_tban`, kick `eventmsg_pm_kick`, mute `eventmsg_pm_mute`, tmute `eventmsg_pm_tmute`, unban
  `eventmsg_pm_unban`, unmute `eventmsg_pm_unmute`, all `Automatic: false` (D-06, D-17).
- **The unmute note.** The `/unmute` site in `mute.go` already reads `mutes_unmute_lockdown_note` during an active lockdown; it also reads
  `eventmsg_pm_unmute_lockdown` there and passes it in `PMNote`. The group default and its `Note` are unchanged.
- **English drafts** (the executor may tighten them inside the limits and the style guide): ban `🚫 You were banned from <b>{group}</b>.`, tban
  `⏳ You were banned from <b>{group}</b> until {until}.`, kick `👢 You were removed from <b>{group}</b>. You can join again.`, mute
  `🔇 You were muted in <b>{group}</b>.`, tmute `🔕 You were muted in <b>{group}</b> until {until}.`, unban `🔓 You were unbanned in <b>{group}</b>.
  You can join again.`, unmute `🔊 You can write in <b>{group}</b> again.`, reason `Reason: {reason}`, lockdown note `The group is in lockdown, so you
  can write once an admin lifts it.`
