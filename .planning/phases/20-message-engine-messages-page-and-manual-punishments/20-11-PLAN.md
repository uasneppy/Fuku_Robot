## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot says in a group when an admin bans, kicks, mutes, unbans or unmutes someone, from that group's settings in the bot's private chat, **so that** the replies sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

This plan proves the five success criteria of the phase end to end and closes its eleven requirements.

A production fix is made only when the test finds a defect, in `event_message.go` or `settings_messages.go`, and is recorded in the SUMMARY.

Purpose: the five success criteria, each as an observable behaviour of the running bot.
Output: one test file, AGENTS.md, the phase SUMMARY with every requirement completed.
</objective>
