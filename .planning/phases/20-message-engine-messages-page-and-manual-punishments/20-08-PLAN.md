## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot says in a group when an admin bans, kicks, mutes, unbans or unmutes someone, from that group's settings in the bot's private chat, **so that** the replies sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

After this plan the owner sets an event's text, media and buttons by sending messages in private, exactly as for Welcome, and the next command posts them.

<objective>
Wire the message editor of plan 20-03 into the event screens (MSG-06, D-20 to D-22): the family's own text part and prompt, the `Remove` and `Write` hooks over three new apply functions, the actions Set, Remove and Cancel through `settingsEditResolvedActions`, the part rows and the hint on the screen, two strings in 8 languages and the AGENTS.md rules.

Screen change (the event screen): the keyboard opens with the editor's part rows, one per part in the order text, media, buttons (`✏️ Set text` and `🗑 Remove text` and their media and buttons siblings, the editor's own labels, each carrying `k`), then the Silent row and Back and Close; the body gains, after the Silent line and a blank line, the family's hint `settings_msg_hint_manual` before the Silent hint. Worst case `[2 2 2 1 2]`, seven rows with plan 20-09's preview rows at most. Button sizes at a 14-character chat ID: Set, Remove and Cancel `a=hs&c=…&k=bn&t=x` 42 bytes.

Purpose: success criterion 2 (set text, one media and URL buttons by sending messages in private, with the editor's limits and refusals) and the change half of criterion 5.
Output: the apply functions, the page wiring, one test file, two strings, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
