## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members with no last name or username, or with names in a script I block, warned, removed or muted automatically, and a muted member able to free themselves once they fix it, **so that** the group screens people when no admin is online.

After this plan the bot can judge a member's names against each rule in microseconds with no Telegram call, pick the strictest punishment of several broken rules, fingerprint a clean member for the pass cache and name a member safely in a group notice.

<objective>
Write the Unicode corpus first, then the pure rules engine that every later plan calls: the rule table, the classifier, the broken-rule mask, the strictest choice, the pass value and the mention (D-04, D-10, D-12, D-13; research S-08; UI-SPEC section 7 "Naming the member").

Purpose: 13-05 and 13-06 draw the page from the rule table; 13-07 punishes with it; 13-08 screens messages with it; 13-11 re-checks "I fixed it" with it.
Output: `fuku/modules/memberreq_rules.go` and its tests.
</objective>
