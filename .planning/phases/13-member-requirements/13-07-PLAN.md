## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members with no last name or username, or with names in a script I block, warned, removed or muted automatically, and a muted member able to free themselves once they fix it, **so that** the group screens people when no admin is online.

After this plan a member who breaks a rule is punished once with the strictest punishment of the rules they break, and the group gets one notice naming them and the rules, with the "I fixed it" button on a mute; nobody is punished on a check that could not be answered.

Output: two production files, the test environment, the fake's member profiles, one test file, AGENTS.md.
