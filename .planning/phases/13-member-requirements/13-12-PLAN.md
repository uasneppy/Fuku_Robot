## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members with no last name or username, or with names in a script I block, warned, removed or muted automatically, and a muted member able to free themselves once they fix it, **so that** the group screens people when no admin is online.

After this plan a kicked member can rejoin and is checked again, a banned one stays out, a muted member's leave and rejoin never strand them muted, and one violation is punished once whether it is seen at the join or at a message.

<objective>
Clear holds at a detected leave, settle them at the rejoin through `MembershipDeps.Reset`, and prove the kick, ban, rejoin and join-plus-message cases (D-07, D-14, D-22; research S-03, Pitfall 5).

Purpose: completes MREQ-04 (the rejoin half of the latch) and MREQ-08; it is split from the join entry (13-11) so each plan stays small.
Output: small edits of membership and greetings, the reset in the join file, the changed test call sites, one test file, the unmute comment, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
