## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members with no last name or username, or with names in a script I block, warned, removed or muted automatically, and a muted member able to free themselves once they fix it, **so that** the group screens people when no admin is online.

After this plan every member is checked when they send a message: a violator is punished once with one notice, whatever replica sees their messages, and a clean member costs no Telegram call.

Purpose: the message half of MREQ-02 and the first end-to-end screening; 13-09 adds deletion and the per-chat stop on this path.
