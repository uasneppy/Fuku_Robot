## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members with no last name or username, or with names in a script I block, warned, removed or muted automatically, and a muted member able to free themselves once they fix it, **so that** the group screens people when no admin is online.

After this plan, with Check on join on, a new member is checked right after the captcha (or on joining when there is none), a member muted on join gets no welcome yet, a kicked member can rejoin and is checked again, and a muted member's leave and rejoin never strand them muted.
