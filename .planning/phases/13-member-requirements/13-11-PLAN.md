## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members with no last name or username, or with names in a script I block, warned, removed or muted automatically, and a muted member able to free themselves once they fix it, **so that** the group screens people when no admin is online.

After this plan a member muted by a rule fixes their profile, taps "I fixed it" and is unmuted (or told what is still missing), gets the welcome that was held back when they were muted on join, and can never use the button to escape anyone else's mute.

Purpose: completes MREQ-06 and MREQ-07.

</objective>
