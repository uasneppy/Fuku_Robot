## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members' commands, late edits and, when I choose, every message removed automatically, **so that** the group stays clean and an edited message cannot slip spam in later.

After this plan the Member messages section is proven safe: only people with the right change it, a Staff Group never sees it, it always says when the bot cannot delete, it reads what the deletion code reads, and it names no Staff Group in any language.

<objective>
Prove the safeguards of the section the previous plan built: the rights (C17-10), the Staff Group exclusion (C17-11), the delete-right line and the cost of a draw (C17-5, C17-6), drift between the page and the three producers and stale screens (C17-15, C17-16), the privacy sweep in 8 languages (C17-16) and the control allow-list and gate doors; record the Permissions and Testing lines of AGENTS.md (D-16, D-17, D-18). The tracer comes first: a real press pipeline where a non-right administrator is refused and the creator is not.
Purpose: the page changes rules that delete people's messages; every door must refuse what it should, and the Staff Group must stay invisible.
Output: one new test file, four extended test files, AGENTS.md (and a production fix only if a test proves one).
</objective>
