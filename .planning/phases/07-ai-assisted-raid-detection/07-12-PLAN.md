## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** have AI flag borderline spam text and images, **so that** subtler raids still trigger a lockdown.

**This slice:** an admin reading `/help lockdown` knows the AI trigger exists, what it sends where, that it never acts on one verdict, and how to switch it off.
