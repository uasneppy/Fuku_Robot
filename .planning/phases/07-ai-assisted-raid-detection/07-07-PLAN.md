## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** have AI flag borderline spam text and images, **so that** subtler raids still trigger a lockdown.

**This slice:** an admin types `/autolockdown ai 8` or `/autolockdown ai off` and the next burst obeys, and `/autolockdown` tells them if the bot has no AI keys.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The AI line follows the flood line in `autolockdownStatusText`, through `autolockdownTriggerLine` with `lockdown_auto_ai_on` and `lockdown_auto_ai_off`; when `raidAIConfigured()` (plan 07-06) is false the notice `lockdown_auto_ai_unavailable` follows the AI line. The other six languages carry natural translations with the same placeholders.
- A new test helper `wantAutoAIRow(aiOn bool, aiN int)` sits beside `wantAutoRow`, so the existing callers stay unchanged.
