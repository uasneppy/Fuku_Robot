<objective>
An image flood from fresh accounts must neither burn the owner's Gemini quota nor hammer a provider that is failing. Before any Gemini call the image job now passes, in order: the album gate (an album is judged once), the fire-latch skip (a lockdown was just attempted, so more checks are wasted), the verdict cache by `file_unique_id` (a repeated sticker or forwarded picture costs nothing), the breakers (per group, and provider-wide for a rejected key or a long 429), and the hourly budgets (150 per group, 500 in all, D-20). Each refusal is no verdict and is counted.

Task 1 is the budgets, the cache, the album gate and the latch skip. Task 2 is the Gemini breakers.

NEVER start PostgreSQL by any means (no su, sudo, setpriv, initdb, pg_ctl, docker). Everything here is Redis or in-process.

Purpose: RAID-04 (bounded cost), RAID-05 (a failing provider changes nothing else), D-07, D-13, D-18, D-20; research Pitfalls 3 and 10. COVERAGE.md row "Error envelope and retry" (the provider-wide pause, Task 2).
Output: the cost guards and breakers in `raid_ai_image.go` and `raid_ai_breaker.go`, two stats counters, tests, AGENTS.md keys.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** have AI flag borderline spam text and images, **so that** subtler raids still trigger a lockdown.

**This slice:** a raid of a thousand stickers costs the owner at most a few hundred Gemini calls an hour, and a broken key or an outage at Google quietly pauses the image checks instead of failing a call per message.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Order inside `raidAIProcessImage`, cheapest and most decisive first: album gate, fire-latch skip, verdict cache, breakers, budgets, then the download and the call. The budget is reserved (INCR) just before the download, so a failed call still spends its unit (the conservative side).
- Keys (D-06 style, through `raidKey` where per chat): album gate `alita:raid:{<chat>}:ag:<media_group_id>` (`SET NX EX 120`; the loser drops its job silently and counts it as cached); per-chat budget `alita:raid:{<chat>}:ab:<yyyymmddhh>` and global budget `alita:raid:aibudget:<yyyymmddhh>` (`INCR`, `EXPIRE` 2 h, UTC hour from `raidNow`); verdict cache `alita:raid:aiv:<file_unique_id>` holding the probability as a decimal string, `SET EX 3600` after a verdict and only for a verdict (no cache of failures or blocks). A Redis error on any of these is no verdict (the image is not sent), never an unbounded call.
- `raidAIChatHourlyBudget = 150` and `raidAIGlobalHourlyBudget = 500` are package variables only so a test can make them small; production never reassigns them (D-20: code constants, env overrides deferred).
- Fire-latch skip: `EXISTS raidKey(chat, raidPartFire)`; set means skip, counted as cached.
- Counters: `raidAICapped` (budget refused) and `raidAICached` (served from the cache, dropped by the album gate or skipped for the latch) with `RaidAIStats.Capped` and `.Cached`. A verdict served from the cache counts Cached and then Spam or Kept like a fresh one, but not Checked, which counts only provider answers that carried a verdict.
- Per-chat Gemini breaker (`raidAIGeminiBreakers`, a mutex-guarded map capped at 10000 chats like `aispamBreakers`): 5 failures pause the chat for `raidAIGeminiCooldown` (5 minutes, a variable for tests); a success clears it. Only Gemini answers count; a `getFile` or download failure does not. No notice is posted anywhere.
- Provider-wide pause (`raidAIGeminiPause`): an `*gemini.HTTPError` with `KeyRejected()` pauses every chat for 5 minutes (`raidAIGeminiKeyPause`); a 429 whose `RetryDelay` is over 2 s pauses for that delay capped at 5 minutes; each pause logs one warning naming its reason (status and code only) and its end.
- Environment: golangci-lint cannot lint Go 1.26 here, so `go vet -tags testtools` and `gofmt -l` stand in for `make lint`.
