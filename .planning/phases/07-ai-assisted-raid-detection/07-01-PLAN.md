## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** have AI flag borderline spam text and images, **so that** subtler raids still trigger a lockdown.

**This slice:** a burst of messages the AI calls spam, from several accounts, locks the group, and the Staff Group's alert says it was the AI trigger.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Redis keys (D-06, discretion): `alita:raid:{<chat>}:av` (verdict items: member = item key `m:<message_id>` or `g:<media_group_id>`, score = report time in unix ms) and `alita:raid:{<chat>}:aa` (accounts: member = user ID, score = time of that account's latest newly-added verdict), both through `raidKey`, so the chat ID is the hash tag. Window 120 s (`raidAIWindow`, fixed), floor 3 accounts (`raidAIMinAccounts`), cap 1000 per set (`raidAICap`, a variable only for tests, never below 500), `PEXPIRE` 5 minutes (`raidAITTL`).
- `ZADD NX` on the item key makes a second report of the same message or album a no-op, and the account is written only when its item was newly added, so a duplicate can never refresh an account whose items aged out (research Pattern 3).
- The rule `raidAICounts(items, accounts, threshold)` also refuses any threshold below `raiddetection.MinThreshold`, so a corrupt setting can never fire on one verdict (research Pitfall 1).
- In this plan `raidAIRecordVerdict` calls `raidFire` synchronously in the caller, which is the `/aispam` worker (drained by `DrainAISpamChecks`); `raidFire` is bounded by `raidFireTimeout` and the 60 s latch makes it at most once per chat per minute. Plan 07-14 moves the call onto a tracked goroutine with its own drain.
- The snapshot key of the spammers is `alita:raid:{<chat>}:aispammers`: the accounts of `:aa` scored by their join time from `:joined` when it is within 24 h, else by their verdict time (an established member whose `/aispam` delete verdict counted, D-19), at most `raidFloodersMax` (500), TTL `raidFloodersTTL` (15 min). `lockdownMergeFlooders` is generalised into one `lockdownMergeSnapshot` with two thin wrappers instead of a copy.
- The counts line of an AI alert (`lockdown_alert_counts_ai`) does not say the listed accounts joined, because some of them may be established members (research Pitfall 13).
- An old replica that wins `BeginAlert` for an `ai_burst` row during a rolling deploy renders the manual wording and no spammer sample (research Pitfall 14); accepted and written into AGENTS.md, like Phase 6's rolling-deploy caveats.
- Environment: golangci-lint cannot lint Go 1.26 here, so `go vet -tags testtools` and `gofmt -l` stand in for `make lint`. The orchestrator runs `make test` after the wave.
