## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** the bot to also tell a punished person in private, from settings I set group by group in the bot's private
chat, **so that** they know what happened and why, while the punishment and the group message are never delayed or changed.

This plan gives the private copy and the group's two options a place to live, with nothing else changed.

<objective>
Storage for Phase 22 (D-07 to D-10): the `pm` destination with its "off unless switched on" default, the per-group options table read with
the messages under the one cached key, their writers, the migration and its tests, the backup exclusion, the harness lists and the AGENTS.md
Data bullet.

Purpose: every later plan of the phase reads the private copy and the options through `eventmsg.Get` and writes them through these functions;
no later plan needs a migration.
Output: one migration, the model, the repository changes, their tests, harness and Makefile entries, AGENTS.md.
</objective>

  updated columns, the inserted row gets `Silent = DefaultSilent(dest)`; the conflict update lists only the writer's own columns, so an existing
