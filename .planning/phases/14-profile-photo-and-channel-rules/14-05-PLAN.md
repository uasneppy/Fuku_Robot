## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** require a profile photo and, in a channel's comment group, a subscription to that channel, **so that** throwaway accounts and outsiders are screened too, without anyone punished because a lookup failed.

After this plan the two rules are real rows of the rule table: an owner sees seven cells, opens the photo or channel rule, picks a punishment (with the same two-tap card for Kick, Ban and Mute) and it is stored in the new columns. The rules do nothing at runtime until plan 14-06 folds them into the executor, so no group is screened by them in the meantime.

<objective>
Append the two rows to the rule table with their `Lookup` functions, make `memberReqBroken` skip them, add `memberReqLookupBroken`, give the photo rule screen its hint, and edit every Phase 13 test that hardcoded five rules to the seven-rule truth (D-13, D-14, D-04, D-01; UI-SPEC amendment M6, section 1, checks 1, 2, 3, 9, 14; research S-01, S-09, S-10).

Purpose: the table drives the page, the controls, the notice phrases and the hold mask, so adding two rows is the one change that makes the new rules reachable everywhere at once, and it breaks every test that counted to five. They are fixed here, together, so no later plan inherits a red test.
Output: the rule table, the page's hint slot, a new `memberReqLookupBroken`, edited Phase 13 tests and new ones, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
