## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** Telegram's service messages and the bot's own messages removed on a schedule I set, **so that** the chat stays readable.

After this plan a service notice (a pin, a new title, a photo change, a joined or left notice) is deleted at once or queued for its time according to a rule stored for the group, in every group but a Staff Group, with joined and left still driven by `/cleanservice`. The worker that carries out queued rows is the next plan.

- `deletionAct` is the one place that decides what happens to a message with a delay: the latch (a `gone` latch stops everything, a `service` latch stops `service:*` kinds), then for delay 0 the Staff Group refusal and an inline delete (or the durable fallback), for delay N the queue row through `deletionSchedule`. `deletionSchedule` is the only caller of `deletion.Schedule` in package `modules` (it adds the latch check and the worker wake); the page, the hooks and the watcher all go through it, so D-07's "scheduling writes nothing for a latched chat" holds for every site.
- The sent time of a message is `deletionSentAt(date)`: `time.Unix(date, 0)` in UTC, and `now` when the date is zero, negative or in the future (clock skew must never stretch a message past its delay, research S-04). Tests set message dates from `deletionNow()` so rows are never in 1970.
- The inline path never retries: a delete that fails with a `retry`-class error is lost (D-08 accepts that one message); a `gone` answer sets the `gone` latch; a refused answer (`message to delete not found`, `message can't be deleted`, any other 4xx that is not 429) is dropped quietly with no log line; a delete the pacer shed (`ratelimit.ErrRateLimited`) takes the same durable fallback as a full inline cap, a row due now plus a wake, which refines D-08 under overload only (research S-05) and is recorded in the SUMMARY.
- The classifier is the research's (S-01): `done`, `retryFree` (`ratelimit.ErrRateLimited` or a cancelled context), `gone` (`chat_status.IsBotMissingError`, descriptions with `group chat was deactivated`, `chat_not_found`, `peer_id_invalid`, `upgraded to a supergroup`, or a non-zero `MigrateToChatId` in the response parameters), `refused` (the two message-level descriptions, `message_id_invalid`, any other 4xx that is not 429 and not gone) and `retry` (everything else). It matches on lower-cased fragments and codes and never on one string; strings with no production history are assumption A1 and the code never depends on them.
- The shared fakes answer every `sendMessage` with `"date":1`; this plan sends no bot message, so the `sendDate` seam of research C-09 belongs to plan 16-05, the first plan that schedules one.
- `deletionContext()` is a root context cancelled by the worker's stop (plan 16-04) exactly as `memberReqContext()` is by its drain; this plan creates it and the helper that restores it for tests (`restoreDeletionWorker`, like `restoreMemberReqRunner`), 16-04 wires the cancel.
