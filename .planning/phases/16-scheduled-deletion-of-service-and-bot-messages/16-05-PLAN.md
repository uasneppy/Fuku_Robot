## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** Telegram's service messages and the bot's own messages removed on a schedule I set, **so that** the chat stays readable.

After this plan the bot's welcome, goodbye, group rules message and the success replies of its manual punishment commands are queued for deletion after the delay stored for their kind, in groups only, and the blacklist's automatic warn is already told apart from a manual one. The remaining automatic notices are the next plan.

<objective>
Add `scheduleBotMessage`, the `sendDate` seam, and the hooks at the welcome, goodbye, group rules and manual punishment reply sites, with the `warnThisUser` source parameter (D-11, D-12, D-13, D-18; research S-04).

Purpose: a stored `bot:*` rule only matters once the sends that own those messages report them to the queue; this plan wires the five sites that need no new machinery and the helper every later hook shares.
Output: one new production file, edits at the named send sites, the fake's seam, two test files, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
