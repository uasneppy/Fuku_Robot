<objective>
Admins can see a lockdown's state and lift it safely. /lockdownstatus shows whether the group is locked, since when, by whom, why, how many joiners were removed, and whether someone reopened the group by hand. /unlockdown replaces a hand-made change, stays locked when the restore fails, and lifts only once when two admins press it together. Anonymous admins go through the existing proof button for all three commands.

Task 1 is the tracer for /lockdownstatus. Task 2 covers the lift's failure and race paths. Task 3 covers anonymous admins.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 4 success criteria 3 (status) and 4 (never lifts on its own, only an admin lifts), and D-12, D-16, D-18, D-19, D-21.
Output: lockdown_status.go, the hardened /unlockdown, chat_status.PromptAnonAdminProof, locale keys, docs and AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** lock my group down during a raid and lift it when safe, **so that** attackers are stopped and settings survive.

**This slice:** an admin can check the lockdown at any time, and a lift never reports success it did not achieve.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- /lockdownstatus checks the viewer live too (creator or any administrator, `requireLockdownAuthority(false)`), not through the admin cache, because the reason and who locked it are only for the group's admins (D-16, D-17).
- The "changed by hand" check compares the live permissions with the stored locked set through `samePermissions`; a failed read never blocks the lift and adds a "couldn't check" line in /lockdownstatus.
- "Removed so far" counts joiner rows in state banned; ban failures and declined join requests are shown on their own lines when non-zero. The rows appear from plan 04-03 on; this plan's tests seed them directly.
- During a lift still unbanning joiners, /lockdownstatus shows the lifter and progress (done of total), and /unlockdown says the lift is already under way.
- `chat_status.PromptAnonAdminProof` is the one new exported function: the proof prompt without the AnonAdmin shortcut. After the proof, `verifyAnonymousAdmin` restores the tapper as the effective sender, so the existing pipeline re-runs `requireLockdownAuthority` live for the tapper.

## Assumptions recorded

- Without Redis the anonymous-admin proof cannot store the original message (existing behaviour of the `alita:anonAdmin:*` keys), so an anonymous admin then has to post as themselves. Lockdown itself works without Redis.

Explicit truths above: LOCK-06 adjacency, empty, ordering; LOCK-07 adjacency, empty; LOCK-02 adjacency; LOCK-04 empty. Flagged assumption (unclassified probe, kept unresolved): LOCK-09 "start a lockdown and see its status" has no shape category; its behaviour is covered by the /lockdownstatus truth here and the /lockdown truths of plan 04-01.
