<objective>
Every way a join can arrive is handled once. The guard also handles join service messages, deduplicates a join that Telegram delivers on both paths by its joiner row, keeps a mixed service message's exempt users for greetings, treats a later re-join as a new join, lets a user in when the chat_member update shows an admin added or approved them, and stops the goodbye message for a joiner the lockdown banned.

Task 1 is the tracer for the service-message path and cross-path deduplication. Task 2 pins down who gets in (the decision table and the performer rules) and the goodbye suppression.

Purpose: LOCK-01 across all join paths, D-03, D-07, D-08, D-10, D-24.
Output: the service-message handler, the kicked-member handler, join-message deletion in the worker, joiner repository additions, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** lock my group down during a raid and lift it when safe, **so that** attackers are stopped and settings survive.

**This slice:** however Telegram reports a join, a raider is removed once and silently, and only people an admin added get in.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Cross-path deduplication is the `(lockdown_id, user_id)` row: the first path to insert decides; a second delivery within `lockdownJoinDedupeWindow` (20 s) of the row's last change is the same join. An older row in a finished state (banned, ban_failed, exempt, declined, decline_failed, cancelled) is a new join and is reclaimed by one conditional update.
- The chat_member update decides with full information (D-24): when it shows a live admin performer and the row is still pending, the row moves to exempt by a conditional update; when the worker already claimed or banned it, the accepted race of D-24 stands and the lift undoes it.
- The service message names only one performer (`from`); an anonymous admin is recognised by `sender_chat` equal to the group (research A4), and on the chat_member path by `from` being the Group Anonymous Bot (1087968824).
- The join service message is deleted only when every user in it was banned, by the worker after the ban, best effort: a failed delete (no delete right) just leaves it (D-10, D-20).
- Goodbye suppression matches the kicked update's until_date against a joiner row's ban_until (2 s tolerance), because `from` is the bot for every bot ban.

## Assumptions recorded

- A3 and A5 (research): the chat_member update for an approved request carries the approving admin in `from`; the service message carries the joiner. Delivery order is unspecified; both orders are tested. Live delivery is a UAT item (human checks below).
- A4 (research): an anonymous admin's service message has `sender_chat` equal to the group.
