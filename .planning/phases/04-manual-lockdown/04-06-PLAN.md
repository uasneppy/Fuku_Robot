<objective>
A lockdown survives anything short of an admin lifting it. A restart mid-lift finishes the remaining unbans from the database; a worker that stopped mid-call leaves claims that any replica takes over after two minutes; a crash between storing a lockdown and Telegram confirming the lock is settled from the live permissions; failed bans are retried and then reported; flushing Redis or losing it entirely changes nothing but pacing; and nothing ends a lockdown on its own.

Task 1 is the tracer for the restart resume. Task 2 covers unconfirmed locks, Redis loss and the never-auto-lift guarantee.

Purpose: LOCK-05 (never lifts on its own, survives restarts and a Redis flush) and the CONTEXT discretion on restarts mid-lift.
Output: stale-claim release, unconfirmed-lock settling, retry accounting tests, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** lock my group down during a raid and lift it when safe, **so that** attackers are stopped and settings survive.

**This slice:** a restart, a crash or a lost Redis never lifts a lockdown or leaves a joiner banned forever.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- A claim (acting or unbanning with claimed_at) older than `lockdownStaleClaim` (2 minutes, far above the 10 s call timeout) belongs to a worker that stopped. Release rules: acting in an active lockdown -> pending (the ban is retried, idempotent); acting in a lifting lockdown -> banned for a ban (the lift's live check decides whether a ban landed) or cancelled for a request; unbanning -> banned (the live check makes a repeat unban safe). No attempt is counted for a release.
- An unconfirmed lockdown (locked_at NULL) older than `lockdownUnconfirmedGrace` (1 minute) is settled by reading the live permissions: equal to the locked set -> ConfirmLocked (the lock took effect, nothing more is announced); different -> DeleteUnconfirmed (the lock never took effect and was never announced). A failed read touches the row and retries after another grace period. The same routine runs synchronously when /lockdown meets an unconfirmed row.
- Without Redis the lockdown keeps working: its state is in PostgreSQL and the pacer falls back to a per-replica interval (research Pattern 6).
