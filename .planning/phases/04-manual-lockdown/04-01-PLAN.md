## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** lock my group down during a raid and lift it when safe, **so that** attackers are stopped and settings survive.

**This slice:** /lockdown mutes everyone but the group's admins without losing a single permission setting, and /unlockdown puts every setting back exactly.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Schema: `chat_lockdowns` (one row per lockdown) and `chat_lockdown_joiners` (one row per user handled during a lockdown) are both created by this plan's single migration, so later plans add no migration. One active lockdown per chat is a partial unique index plus an `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` insert (groundwork for LOCK-10). `trigger_kind` has no CHECK so Phase 6 can add kinds without a migration.
- Joiner columns answer research Open Question 7 now: `first_name`, `username`, `is_bot`, `join_path`, `invite_link`, `via_join_request` and `performer_id` are stored for Phase 5's alert sample and its "Revoke link" (LOCK-13). Adding them after merge would need a new migration.
- The snapshot is the raw `permissions` member of a `getChat` answer, read through `bot.RequestWithContext` and replayed verbatim (research Pattern 1). The lock sends a constant with all 16 keys explicit false.
- Lockdowns need a supergroup (research A6, Pitfall 9): a basic group is refused with an upgrade hint, because a ban there does not keep anyone out.
- Lockdown reads are fresh queries, never cached (the staff audit-record precedent), so no cache invalidation applies and every replica sees a new lockdown at once.
- `/lockdown` and `/unlockdown` are not `Disableable`: a chat cannot switch off its raid tool.
- Authority is checked inside the pipeline by `requireLockdownAuthority(true)`, a live `getChatMember` with a 5 s timeout, never the admin cache (D-11). Anonymous admins are handled in plan 04-02.
- `/unlockdown` finishes the lift at once when the lockdown has no unfinished joiner rows (`FinishLift`); plan 04-03's worker finishes the others.
- The reason is cut to 300 runes and the name to 64 runes, stored raw, HTML-escaped at render and spliced in after translation through tokens, because the translator runs a printf-style pass over interpolated text.
