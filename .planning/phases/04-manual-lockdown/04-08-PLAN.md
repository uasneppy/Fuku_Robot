## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin, **I want to** lock my group down during a raid and lift it when safe, **so that** attackers are stopped and settings survive.

**This slice:** staff can see a locked group from the Staff Group, and their bans survive the lift.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The marker uses the lock's confirmation time (`locked_at`), formatted "2 Jan 15:04" in UTC like the history lines, as one extra line after the row's status line. One batch query per panel build keeps `renderStaffPanel` pure; a failed query shows the panel without markers.
- `LockdownBan` is appended as the last field of `staffTargetState` (every literal in the repository is keyed, research Open Question 3), set only for a staff ban on a kicked target with a non-zero end date, so no other kind pays a query.
- The staff ban that replaces a lockdown ban may end sooner than 330 days; that is the point: it becomes a deliberate ban with its own end date, and the lift keeps it. The undo of such a staff ban restores the lockdown's marker ban, which the lift then lifts (research Pitfall 4, "Staff undo").
