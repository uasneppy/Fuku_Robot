<objective>
The alert reaches its second destination and survives a bad day. The log channel of the locked group gets its own copy, under the usual log header, when that channel's admin category is on, and its Lift button works like the Staff Group's. Every copy is delivered by any replica, retried on failure, and resumed after a crash.

Task 1 is the log channel copy. Task 2 is delivery: retries, rate limits, stale claims, concurrent cycles and the settle path.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criteria 1 and 2 (log channel half), LOCK-11, LOCK-06, D-01, D-20.
Output: changes to lockdown_alert.go, lockdown_worker.go and alerts.go, a delivery test file, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** the alert also lands in the group's log channel, and it arrives even when Telegram or a replica fails along the way.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The log channel destination and its header come from `actionlog.Destination(chat, logchannels.CategoryAdmin)`, the same resolver staff log posts use, so D-20's category switch and the header live in one place. The copy's stored body includes the header.
- A copy claimed (`posting`) for longer than `lockdownStaleClaim` (2 minutes) is given back without counting an attempt, and a posted copy whose close claim is that old loses the claim. A crash between the send and the record can therefore post a second copy; a duplicate alert is harmless, a lost one is not (research Pattern 2).
- Destinations are decided once, at the gate; a Staff Group linked or a log channel set after the alert was decided gets no copy of that alert.
