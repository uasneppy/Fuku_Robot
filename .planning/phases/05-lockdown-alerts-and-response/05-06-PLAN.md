<objective>
Finish "Ban N recent joiners" as a tool staff can rely on during a long raid. The button keeps working until the lift, each press bans only what no earlier press handled, one run goes at a time, a restart resumes it and a lift stops it. The result says exactly what happened, account by account where it failed, and the group's log channel keeps one record per run.

Task 1 is the run's lifecycle. Task 2 is the report and the log post.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 3 ("told how many were banned, skipped and failed"), LOCK-12, D-11, D-12, D-21, D-22.
Output: changes to lockdown_response.go, lockdown_alert_callbacks.go and burst.go, a test file, locale keys, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** staff can press Ban again as the raid goes on, and always see what each press did.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- A run whose lockdown is no longer active is stopped by the worker: its queued accounts go back to unhandled (state '', run_id NULL), it moves to `stopped` through the same conditional finish, and its card says it stopped. The lift has already closed the alert's buttons (plan 05-01), so nobody can press again.
- "Handled" means a burst row in a terminal state (banned, skipped, failed). Approved users are never queued and stay unhandled, so a later press includes them if their approval was removed.
- The result lists failed accounts by name, ID and Telegram's stored reason, up to 10, then "and N more", spliced in after translation. Skipped accounts are counted per reason only.
- The #LOCKDOWN_BAN post is one per run, sent by the replica whose `FinishBanRun` won, through `actionlog.Destination(chat, CategoryAdmin)` and the lockdown pacer; like staff log posts, a failed post is logged and changes nothing. Its hashtag is not localized.
