<objective>
The alert shows the raid itself. When the alert is decided, the tracker's joins from the 10 minutes before the lock are saved as the lockdown's burst rows in PostgreSQL, so nothing later needs Redis; the alert then lists up to 10 accounts of the burst (pre-lockdown joiners, then the joiners the lockdown recorded) with the counts behind the trigger.

Task 1 is the end-to-end path: a pre-lockdown joiner appears on the alert. Task 2 completes the sample.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 1 ("the counts behind it, and a sample of the accounts that joined during the burst, including those removed"), LOCK-11, LOCK-01, D-02, D-03, D-17.
Output: migration 20261011130000, the burst model and repository, the sample in the alert, locale keys, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** the alert names the accounts of the burst, including the ones who got in just before the lock.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The snapshot window is (locked_at minus 10 minutes, locked_at]. It is read in the worker's plan step just before `BeginAlert` and saved as burst rows (source `pre`) in the same transaction; joins later than locked_at are the lockdown's own joiner rows.
- `chat_lockdown_burst` is created with its whole row shape, including the per-account response columns (state, reason, detail, run_id, attempts, claimed_at) that "Ban N recent joiners" moves in plans 05-08 and 05-10, so every statement stays an idempotent CREATE.
- The sample shows pre-lockdown rows first (by join time), then joiner rows in recording order, users with a pre row not repeated; names are plain escaped text (no mention links) with @username and the ID in code tags; bots get a localized mark. D-02's burst is literal: every joiner row the lockdown recorded is part of the sample, whatever its state (D-22 only concerns "Ban N recent joiners").
- Test rows: the sample tests' helper deletes the burst rows of its chats at cleanup.
