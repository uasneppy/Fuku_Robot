<objective>
The alert shows how the raiders got in, and an admin of the locked group shuts that door. The link most of the burst used is shown with its count and the number of other links; a link the bot created can be revoked from the alert behind a confirm card, by a live creator or an administrator with the invite right; a link another admin created is shown with a pointer to Telegram's own settings, because the bot cannot revoke it. After a revoke every live copy says who revoked the link.

Task 1 is the link line. Task 2 is the Revoke path end to end. Task 3 is every refusal.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 4, LOCK-13, D-13, D-14, D-18.
Output: migration 20261011150000, the top-link query, revoke record and copy sync, the button and card, the fake's revokeChatInviteLink, locale keys, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** staff see which invite link the raid used, and an admin of the group revokes it from the alert when the bot can.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- The link counts come from the burst: pre-lockdown rows and the joiner rows of users with no pre row, grouped by `invite_link`, most used first, ties by link. Join requests' links count like joins.
- A link is revocable when it is non-empty and has no "..." or "…" in it (research F2, D-18: other admins' links reach the bot cut short). The link is stored on the copy's row when the copy is posted, and the Revoke handler only ever revokes that stored link.
- One revoke is recorded per lockdown (`link_revoked_at IS NULL` conditional update). After it, no copy shows a Revoke button.
- Live copies are refreshed by a generic worker step: `RecordLinkRevoked` marks every posted, open copy that still has buttons `edit_pending`, and `lockdownSyncAlertCopies` re-edits each to its stored body plus footers with its current keyboard. Plan 05-08 reuses the step to take the buttons off older reminders.
- The bot's own invite right is not pre-checked (research A3): Telegram decides, and its refusal is mapped to a plain sentence (no rights, link not found or not the bot's, rate limited, other).
- Revoke authority is the invite right (D-14), not the restrict right: `lockdownInviteAuthority` is a live getChatMember of the lockdown row's chat (creator, or administrator with can_invite_users); a failed lookup refuses.

## Assumptions recorded

- A4 and A7 (research): Telegram's exact error text for revoking a link the bot did not create, and whether a bot-created link arrives in full in chat_member updates, are confirmed in UAT; the mapping falls back to the generic line.
