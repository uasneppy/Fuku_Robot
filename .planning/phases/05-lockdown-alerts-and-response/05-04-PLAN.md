<objective>
The alert shows the raid itself. A dedicated Redis tracker remembers each group's joins from the last 10 minutes, written best effort by the group -7 join handlers. When the alert is decided, the joins from the 10 minutes before the lock are saved as the lockdown's burst rows in PostgreSQL, and the alert lists up to 10 accounts of the burst (pre-lockdown joiners, then the joiners the lockdown recorded) with the counts behind the trigger.

Task 1 is the end-to-end path: a pre-lockdown joiner appears on the alert. Task 2 completes the sample and pins the tracker's limits.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 1 ("the counts behind it, and a sample of the accounts that joined during the burst, including those removed"), LOCK-11, LOCK-01, D-02, D-03, D-17.
Output: migration 20261011130000, the burst model and repository, lockdown_recent.go, guard hooks, the sample in the alert, locale keys, AGENTS.md rules.

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** the alert names the accounts of the burst, including the ones who got in just before the lock.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Tracker (D-17): key `alita:lockdown:recent:<chat>`, a sorted set scored by join time in unix milliseconds, member a compact JSON `{i: id, n: name, u: username, b: bot, l: link}`. One pipelined round trip per join: ZADD for each user, ZREMRANGEBYSCORE older than 10 minutes, ZREMRANGEBYRANK keeping the newest 500, EXPIRE 700 s, under a 250 ms timeout. The key is in the operational `alita:lockdown:*` family, outside `alita:cache:`, so `CLEAR_CACHE_ON_STARTUP` keeps it. Join requests are not tracked: a request is not a join.
- Deduplication across the two join paths happens at read time by user ID (the earliest join time wins, a non-empty link or username fills in), because the chat_member path carries the invite link and the service message does not.
- The snapshot window is (locked_at minus 10 minutes, locked_at]. It is read in the worker's plan step just before `BeginAlert` and saved as burst rows (source `pre`) in the same transaction; joins later than locked_at are the lockdown's own joiner rows.
- `chat_lockdown_burst` is created with its whole row shape, including the per-account response columns (state, reason, detail, run_id, attempts, claimed_at) that "Ban N recent joiners" moves in plan 05-05, so every statement stays an idempotent CREATE.
- The sample shows pre-lockdown rows first (by join time), then joiner rows in recording order, users with a pre row not repeated; names are plain escaped text (no mention links) with @username and the ID in code tags; bots get a localized mark.
- D-02's burst is literal: every joiner row the lockdown recorded is part of it, whatever its state.
