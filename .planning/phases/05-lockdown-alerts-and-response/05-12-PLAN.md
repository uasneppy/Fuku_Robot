<objective>
An admin of the locked group shuts the door the raid came through. A link the bot created gets a "Revoke link" button on the alert; a live creator or an administrator with the invite right revokes it behind a confirm card; the revoke is recorded once and every live copy then says who revoked the link. Every refusal, by authority or by Telegram, is answered in plain words with nothing changed.

Task 1 is the Revoke path end to end. Task 2 is every refusal.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 4 ("Only an admin of that group can revoke it with 'Revoke link'"), LOCK-13, D-14, D-18.
Output: migration 20261011150000, the revoke record and copy sync, the button and card, the fake's revokeChatInviteLink, locale keys, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** an admin of the group revokes the raid's invite link from the alert when the bot can.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- One revoke is recorded per lockdown (`link_revoked_at IS NULL` conditional update). After it, no copy shows a Revoke button.
- Live copies are refreshed by a generic worker step: `RecordLinkRevoked` marks every posted, open copy that still has buttons `edit_pending`, and `lockdownSyncAlertCopies` re-edits each to its stored body plus footers with its current keyboard. Plan 05-13 reuses the step to take the buttons off older reminders.
- The bot's own invite right is not pre-checked (research A3): Telegram decides, and its refusal is mapped to a plain sentence (no rights, link not found or not the bot's, rate limited, other).
- Revoke authority is the invite right (D-14), not the restrict right: `lockdownInviteAuthority` is a live getChatMember of the lockdown row's chat (creator, or administrator with can_invite_users); a failed lookup refuses.
- The Revoke handler only ever revokes the link stored on the pressed copy (plan 05-09), and a copy whose stored link is not revocable never has the button.

## Assumptions recorded

- A4 (research): Telegram's exact error text for revoking a link the bot did not create is confirmed in UAT; the mapping falls back to the generic line.
