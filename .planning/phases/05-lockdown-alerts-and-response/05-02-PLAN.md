<objective>
An admin of the locked group lifts the lockdown from the alert. The alert gets a "Lift lockdown" button; the press is checked live against the locked group, a confirm card only the presser can use replies to the alert, and Confirm runs the exact /unlockdown path through one shared lift function, so the restore-then-record order, the one-winner record and the tally are the same. The group sees the same public lift notice as after /unlockdown.

Task 1 is the end-to-end lift from the Staff Group. Task 2 covers every other outcome of that lift and keeps /unlockdown unchanged.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 2, LOCK-06 (alert button), LOCK-11 (Lift button on the alert), D-05, D-06, D-08, D-19.
Output: liftLockdownRow in lockdown.go, the Lift button, lockdown_alert_callbacks.go, a test file, locale keys, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** an admin of the locked group lifts it from the Staff Group with one button and a confirmation.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Confirm cards are stateless: Confirm and Cancel carry the asker's user ID (`u`) and an expiry in unix minutes (`e`) inside the callback data, so the buttons work with Redis down (research F4, Pattern 4). Every authority fact is checked live again at Confirm. This plan writes `e`; plan 05-04 enforces it.
- Callback data: Ask `lockdown|v1|a=lk&r=<copy row id>`, Confirm `a=lc&e=..&r=..&u=..`, Cancel `a=x&r=..&u=..`. The handler loads the copy row, then its lockdown, and takes the locked chat from the lockdown row; the namespace does not start with `antiraid`.
- `liftLockdownRow` is the body of /unlockdown from the manual-change read to FinishLift, moved unchanged. The AGENTS.md rule that `BeginLift` is reached only through `lockdownBeginLift` in `unlockdown` now names `liftLockdownRow`.
- Interactive answers (the card, its edits, toasts) are sent directly like the staff cards; the public lift notice into the locked group goes through `lockdownPaced`. Toast texts are plain; card texts are HTML with user text spliced after translation.

## Assumptions recorded

- A1 (research): a callback from a button on a bot post carries the real presser in `from`. UAT presses from the Staff Group (and, after plan 05-06, from the log channel).
