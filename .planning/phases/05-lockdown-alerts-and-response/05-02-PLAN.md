Every way to press it without the right is refused before anything happens, and the confirm card behaves like the staff cards: only the asker can use it, it expires, and a second Confirm changes nothing. Task 1 is the authority matrix. Task 2 is the card lifecycle and the callback data.

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** only a live admin of the locked group can ever lift it from an alert, whoever else can see the button.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Service identities (`staffServiceUserIDs`: Telegram 777000, the Group Anonymous Bot, the Channel Bot) are refused with "post as yourself" before any database read or Telegram call, as the staff Undo does. A callback's `from` is the real presser, so this is defence in depth for D-05's anonymous-admin and channel-identity cases.
- A copy row is honoured only on the chat it was posted in (`slot.DestChatID` equals the pressed message's chat), like the staff history detail view; a mismatch is answered as no longer valid. Authority would refuse anyway, but a forged or replayed press then never reaches a lookup.
- An administrator without `can_restrict_members` gets its own refusal text, as `/unlockdown` distinguishes `chat_status_restrict_cmd_error` from the admin refusal.
- Expiry is lazy: `e` is unix minutes; a Confirm after it edits the card to "expired". No timer and no Redis key (research Pattern 4).
