<objective>
Every copy of the alert reflects the lift and no stale button can do anything. Once a lockdown leaves the active state, the worker edits each posted copy to its stored text plus "Lifted by X at T" and removes its buttons; a press on a button of a lifted lockdown is answered "Already lifted" and closes that copy at once. The repository's gate, copy claims and close claims get their own tests.

Task 1 closes every copy at the lift and adds the repository tests. Task 2 makes stale buttons harmless.

Purpose: ROADMAP Phase 5 success criterion 2 ("If the lockdown has already been lifted, pressing a button on its alert says so and does nothing"), LOCK-06, D-04, D-07.
Output: close claims in alerts.go, the repository test file, the close step and footer in lockdown_alert.go, the stale path in the callbacks, the worker hook, one locale key, AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** group admin or staff member, **I want to** get a lockdown alert with one-tap fixes, **so that** we can see a raid and clean it up fast.

**This slice:** after a lift every alert says who lifted it and when, and its buttons are gone.

## Decisions taken here (Claude's discretion, recorded)

- Closing is a worker step (research Pattern 8), so it also covers /unlockdown and resumes after a restart. It runs as soon as the lockdown is no longer active (lifting or lifted), newest copies first, at most `lockdownAlertBatch` per cycle so `StopLockdownWorker`'s 5 s wait holds (research Pitfall 5).
- The close claim reuses the copy's `claimed_at` (posted rows are not claimed otherwise); a stale close claim is released by plan 05-06's stale step.
- "Message to edit not found", "message can't be edited" and MESSAGE_ID_INVALID close the copy (it is gone); "message is not modified" is success.
- The stale-button path runs before any action: a lockdown that is lifting or lifted is answered and nothing else runs, so the chat's current lockdown is never looked up (research Pitfall 1).
