## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** accounts whose names look like spam flagged by AI, **so that** advertising accounts are screened when they join, with no message text leaving the server.

After this plan a member held for a spam-like name is told in the group what to do, and a member who changed their name can free themselves with the button, while a member who did not is told what is still wrong and an AI that cannot answer changes nothing.

Purpose: a punishment the member cannot understand or escape is a bug in a public message; this plan is where the AI rule's words and its escape hatch meet the member.
Output: three edited production files, two new test files, three edited test files, AGENTS.md.
