<objective>
Make "Undo everywhere" safe under every way people and replicas actually press buttons: the card lifecycle (Cancel, presser-only, expiry, double tap), every refusal (outsider, service identity, forged or stale record, kick, Redis down, presser leaving), one undo per action across cards and replicas, the shared per-target lock, a shutdown in the middle of an undo, and no time limit.

Task 1 covers the card lifecycle and the refusals. Task 2 covers exactly-once, the lock, shutdown and D-05, and records the drain and lock rules in AGENTS.md.

Purpose: STAFF-11 ("someone outside the Staff Group can't undo anything"), D-05, D-06, D-07, D-09.
Output: refusal branches and presser-only wording, one locale key in 7 locales, a lifecycle test file and AGENTS.md rules.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** log, list and undo every staff action, **so that** mistakes can be reversed and we stay accountable.

**This slice:** an undo happens once, only by the member who confirmed it, never alongside another action on the same person, and survives a restart truthfully.

## Assumptions recorded

- A callback query's From is the real pressing user, also for anonymous admins (research A4). The staffServiceUserIDs refusal is defence in depth.
