<objective>
Close the first half of gap G-03-4 (WR-01), as the owner chose in UAT test 4 (option b): an undo whose run reached no Telegram write in any group gives the one-undo claim back, so a staff member with rights can retry; once any group reached its write, D-09 (one undo per action) holds as before. Purpose: today staffUndoConfirm takes the latch and marks the original "Undone" before any group is visited, and nothing ever releases it (debug session T1, T2, T4). A member who is an admin nowhere can spend the only undo of an action with no effect.
Output: ClaimUndo returning its claim time, ReleaseUndo, a per-group Reached marker, the new Finish and Delivered hooks of the undo run, two locale keys and help text in 7 languages, regenerated docs, AGENTS.md rules, and regression tests.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** log, list and undo every staff action, **so that** mistakes can be reversed and we stay accountable.

**This slice:** an undo that could not change anything no longer uses up the action's only undo, and the original summary says "Undone" only after something was actually undone.
