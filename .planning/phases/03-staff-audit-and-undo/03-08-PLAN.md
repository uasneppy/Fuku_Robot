<objective>
Prove, end to end against the stateful fake, that "Undo everywhere" restores exactly what was there before in every case the decision table covers, never overrules a later decision, touches only applied and still-linked groups, and acts only where the presser has rights. Then log each successful undo to the group's log channel, under the same rules as the action itself.

Task 2 adds the #STAFF_UNDO log post and its tests, and records the rule in AGENTS.md.

Output: fake extension, two test files, the undo log composition and hook, one locale key in 7 locales, the AGENTS.md rule.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** staff member, **I want to** log, list and undo every staff action, **so that** mistakes can be reversed and we stay accountable.

**This slice:** an undo leaves each group exactly as it was before the staff action, and each group's own admins see the undo in their log channel.

- Live Telegram behaviour of restrictChatMember on a kicked or left target, and of use_independent_chat_permissions, is modelled by the fake from the gotgbot doc comments; it cannot be proven here (research A2, A3). The live checks are listed in 03-VALIDATION.md "Manual-Only Verifications" and in plan 03-09's end-of-phase human checks.
