<objective>
The hourly background recheck (D-15.2). It is what makes "the link disappears without anyone running a command" hold for groups where the bot is not an admin, or where no ownership update was delivered. It also runs shortly after startup, because the Bot API drops member updates older than 24 hours. With several replicas only one runs each cycle (Redis lock), and it stays correct without Redis. It reuses `recheckStaffGroup`, `recheckLink` and `applyLinkHealth`, so the notices are identical and posted exactly once.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3 (SETUP-06). PLAT-01 applies here even though it is mapped to Phase 2 (CONTEXT Claude's Discretion).
Output: `staff_sweeper.go`, `ListStaffGroupsFresh`/`DeleteOrphanLinks`, main.go start/stop wiring, and AGENTS.md documenting `alita:staff:*`.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** stale links disappear on their own every hour, even where the bot can't see ownership updates.

## Assumptions recorded

- A2: `getChatAdministrators` works for a bot that is not an admin and lists the creator. If it doesn't, those links stay unknown (never removed) until the bot is admin. The end-of-phase human check in Task 2 verifies this.
- Claude's Discretion "the hourly re-check may clean up a Staff Group that Telegram says no longer exists": declined, because deleting on an API error contradicts P4. Only orphan links (whose Staff Group row is gone) are cleaned.
- The lock is not released early. Its TTL (interval minus one minute) means one sweep per hour across all replicas.
