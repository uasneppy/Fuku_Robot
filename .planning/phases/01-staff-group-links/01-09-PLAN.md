<objective>
The live `/staff` panel (SETUP-08, D-17, D-18, D-19, D-20). Opening `/staff` or pressing Refresh checks every link live: the owner side through `recheckLink` and the bot side through `FetchBotMember` plus `applyLinkHealth`. It renders one message that is edited in place. It pages cleanly at 8 groups and never exceeds Telegram's size limit in any locale. Any Staff Group member can open it and refresh it; Add group and Unlink remain owner-only (plans 01-05 and 01-06).

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 5. The in-lockdown status is added in Phase 4.
Output: `buildStaffPanelRows`, the final `renderStaffPanel` (statuses, legend, updated time, paging, length cap), and the Refresh and Page callback actions.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** staff see the live health of every linked group in `/staff` and can refresh it in place.

## Assumptions recorded

- A9: Telegram's "message is not modified" 400 is matched case-insensitively on the error text and treated as success. The "Updated" line normally makes each edit differ anyway.
- The panel does not re-key chats. It runs inside the live Staff Group chat, and linked groups are supergroups. The 400 migrate detector runs from the Staff Group recheck (plan 01-07) and the sweep (plan 01-10).
- Panel status icons are emoji (not localized). Every word, including the legend and the reasons, is localized.
- The seven locale files are one mechanical edit per task.
