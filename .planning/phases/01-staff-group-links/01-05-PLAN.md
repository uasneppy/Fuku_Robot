<objective>
Linking (SETUP-03, SETUP-04). An owner of both groups links a supergroup to their Staff Group, either by typing `/linkstaff [id]` in the group (D-02) or with the "Add group" button that opens Telegram's group picker (D-01). Both entry points run one shared `runLinkGroup` core with every D-03 check done live. The link is created even when the bot lacks rights, with a warning (D-04). Confirmations and owner-facing refusals go only to the Staff Group (D-06), and the command message is deleted from the linked group (D-07).

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2.
Output: `staff_link.go`, `staff_notify.go`, the group deep-link registry, the help.go `/start` group branch, the link repository functions, the Add group button, and a `/staff` that lists linked groups.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** the owner of both groups links a group to the Staff Group, typed or through the picker.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions)

- OQ2: anonymous senders get "post as yourself", via the shared `helpers.IsAnonymousSender` predicate from plan 01-02. The reply here deletes itself so the linked group stays clean.
- OQ6 (Claude's Discretion "Where refusals go"): replies in the issuing group delete themselves with `time.AfterFunc(staffSelfDeleteAfter)` (30s, the captcha.go:1329 precedent). A restart loses the timer and leaves a short refusal visible, which is accepted.
- Picker payload format (Claude's Discretion): `stf_` + the decimal of `-staffChatID`. It is at most 24 characters, uses only the charset `A-Za-z0-9_-`, has no expiry, and grants no authority. The live checks decide.
- A3, A12, A4: whether the official clients accept a group the bot is already in, keep the bot's existing rights, and produce anonymous senders. Covered by the end-of-phase human check in Task 3. `/linkstaff` is the fallback if the picker misbehaves.
- When the bot-status lookup itself fails (unknown), the link is stored with health `ok` and no warning. The live panel (plan 01-09) and the sweep (plan 01-10) correct it.
- The seven locale files are one mechanical edit per task.
- Task-count note: this plan has 3 tasks (one tracer and two expansion tasks), inside the 2-3 target. `verify.plan-structure` reports task_count 3; a raw substring count of the task tag also matches the enclosing tasks wrapper element, so it reads one higher.
