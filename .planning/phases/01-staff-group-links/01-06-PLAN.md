<objective>
Unlinking (SETUP-05, D-05). The owner of both groups can unlink a group by typing `/unlinkstaff` in it, or with the Unlink button next to it in `/staff`. The button asks "Are you sure?" and re-checks live on every press. Notices go only to the Staff Group (D-06), and the linked group keeps no trace (D-07).

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 2 ("can link and unlink").
Output: `staff_unlink.go` with the shared `runUnlinkGroup` core, `/unlinkstaff`, the `ul`/`uc`/`ux` callback actions, per-row Unlink buttons in the panel, and `GetLinkByIDFresh`/`DeleteLink`.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** the owner of both groups unlinks a group, typed or with a button.

## Assumptions recorded

- Manual unlink is allowed for whoever is the live creator of both groups, even if they are not the original link maker. Under D-12 such a link is already invalid, and the recheck (plan 01-07) would remove it anyway.
- The seven locale files are one mechanical edit per task.

| T-01-SC | Tampering | npm/pip/cargo installs | low | accept | No package installs; `git diff --exit-code go.mod go.sum` |
</threat_model>
