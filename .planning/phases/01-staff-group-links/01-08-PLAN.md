<objective>
Bot-health tracking for links (D-14). Losing the bot never breaks a link. The link's health changes, and the Staff Group gets one heads-up per change, including recovery when the bot is added back. `/staff` (plan 01-09) and the hourly sweep (plan 01-10) reuse this transition primitive.

Purpose: SETUP-08's "bot is admin" and "bot can restrict" statuses need a durable, change-only signal, and D-14 forbids a notice on every check.
Output: `staff.SetLinkHealth`, `applyLinkHealth`, the `my_chat_member` watcher at group -3, and heads-up strings.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** the Staff Group is told once when the bot loses, or regains, what it needs in a linked group.

## Assumptions recorded

- Claude's Discretion "The bot is removed from the Staff Group, or loses admin there": keep the status and links and do nothing. No heads-up is possible there anyway. The sweep never deletes a Staff Group because of API errors (P4).
- The health is derived from the `my_chat_member` update's own `new_chat_member`, which is Telegram's statement about the bot, so no extra API call is made.
- The seven locale files are one mechanical edit per task.
