<objective>
SETUP-07: a Staff Group keeps its status and links when its chat ID changes. Telegram's `migrate_to_chat_id` and `migrate_from_chat_id` service messages, and the 400 `migrate_to_chat_id` error parameter, each trigger one idempotent re-key transaction. The re-key also covers a linked group's ID (D-11, Claude's Discretion: cheap and harmless).

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 4. Nothing in the codebase handles chat migration today.
Output: `staff.RekeyChat`, the new non-help `StaffWatchers` module at handler group `-3` (later plans 01-07 and 01-08 add the ownership and bot-health watchers to it), `rekeyFromTelegramError` (plans 01-09 and 01-10 wire it into the panel and sweeper), and AGENTS.md documenting group `-3`.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** a Staff Group (and its links) survives a group-to-supergroup upgrade.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions)

- OQ1: `botJoinedGroup` leaves basic groups, so a basic-group Staff Group is practically unreachable (RESEARCH C2). SETUP-07 is verified with repository tests and synthetic `migrate_*` messages, with no live upgrade UAT. The code path stays because D-11 and SETUP-07 require it.
- A7: the arrival order of the two migrate messages is unspecified, so one idempotent `RekeyChat` handles both orders.
- The re-key is silent: no notice is posted (no requirement asks for one). It is logged at info.
