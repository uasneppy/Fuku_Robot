<objective>
Automatic unlinking when ownership changes (SETUP-06, D-12, D-13, D-15.1, D-15.3). One recheck core (`recheckLink` and `recheckStaffGroup`) is fed by event triggers here: the dedicated `chat_owner_changed`/`chat_owner_left` service messages, which need no bot admin rights, and `chat_member` creator transitions. The panel (plan 01-09) and the hourly sweep (plan 01-10) reuse the same core, and Phase 2 calls `recheckLink` before every staff action. Only a definite live mismatch removes a link. Exactly one Staff Group notice is posted, however many triggers race.

Purpose: ROADMAP success criterion 3, "the link disappears without anyone running a command".
Output: `staff_recheck.go`, the ownership watchers in the StaffWatchers module (group -3), `DeleteLinkIfOwner`/`UpdateStaffGroupOwner`, notice strings, and an AGENTS.md trap.
</objective>

## Phase Goal

**As a** community owner, **I want to** make one group my Staff Group and link my groups to it, **so that** staff have one control room.

**This slice:** a link disappears by itself as soon as the person who made it no longer owns both groups.

## Assumptions recorded (research Open Questions)

- OQ3 / A1: whether Telegram emits `chat_member` on an ownership transfer is undocumented. The `chat_member` watcher is an extra; the service messages, the sweep (plan 01-10) and the live checks are the guarantee. The end-of-phase human check in Task 2 resolves this.
- A5: `chat_owner_changed`/`chat_owner_left` reach a bot that is only a member. If they don't, the sweep still catches the change.
- OQ5 (recommended default): `staff_groups.owner_user_id` is refreshed from the live creator on every Staff Group recheck. It is only a lookup hint for `/linkstaff` with no argument and never an authority.
- Claude's Discretion "The Staff Group changes owner": all links made by the previous owner break, Staff status stays with the group, and a notice goes to the Staff Group.
- The seven locale files are one mechanical edit per task.

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Telegram updates to watchers | Service messages and chat_member updates can be late, missing, reordered, or about unrelated chats |
| Several replicas and triggers to one link row | The watchers, the panel and the sweep may recheck the same link at the same moment |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-22 | Elevation | A stale link granting a former owner power | high | mitigate | Live `CheckOwner` on four triggers (service messages, chat_member, panel, sweep); D-12 maker-must-own-both rule in `recheckLink` and `recheckStaffGroup` |
| T-01-23 | Spoofing / Tampering | A trusted update payload (NewOwner) or a chat_member update for the wrong chat | medium | mitigate | The payload is only a hint; the decision comes from the live admin list; cached gates filter unrelated chats (TestStaffOwnershipPayloadIsOnlyAHint, TestStaffOwnershipChatMemberCreatorTransition) |
