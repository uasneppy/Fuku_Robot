## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** warnings, reports, language, the log channel, anonymous-admin mode and locks on menu pages, **so that** I can configure them without commands.

After this plan the group's log categories are turned on or off from a Log channel page exactly as `/log` and `/nolog` turn them, with the same audit line, and the page never reveals which channel the log goes to.
