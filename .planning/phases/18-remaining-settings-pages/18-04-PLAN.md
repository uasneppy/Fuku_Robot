## Planner assumptions and deviation notes (flagged)

- Execution rules, the golden technique, the reader rule and `$SCRATCH` are as 18-01's planner assumptions say.
- **Owner refusal key (D-15, planner's choice).** D-15 leaves the key of the non-owner refusal to the planner, "checked against what the press pipeline already uses". The press is a callback query, and every command that refuses a non-owner (including `/anonadmin`) answers through `chat_status.NewPermissionResponder(...).Respond(ctx, "chat_status_owner_cmd_error", "chat_status_owner_button_error", ...)`, which picks the `_button_error` variant for a callback. So the right refuses with **`chat_status_owner_button_error`** ("You need to be the group creator to do this!"), an alert, as the command's own responder would answer a button. This differs from the UI-SPEC's `chat_status_owner_cmd_error` on purpose and only in the key; both exist in all 8 locales. `settingsRightOwner` (the Access page) keeps `settings_access_owner_only`.
- **Order of a composite right (D-16).** `settingsRightRefusal` today judges the member part first and the bot part after. The two new rights judge the bot part first (the commands' order: `/autoapprove`'s `IsUserConnected(true, true)` and `/anonadmin`'s `RequireBotAdmin` in the pipeline both run before the user right), then the member part through `settingsRightRefusalFor`. The six existing rights keep their order exactly; the existing cases of `TestSettingsRights` and `TestSettingsRightRefusalForParity` stay as they are.
- `settingsRightRefusalFor` (pure, shared with the message editor's typed reply) gains the two member parts: `settingsRightChangeInfoBotAdmin` answers what `settingsRightChangeInfo` answers, `settingsRightOwnerBotAdmin` answers `chat_status_owner_button_error` (alert) for anyone but the creator. No editor part declares the new rights, so the typed-reply door's behaviour is unchanged.
- The rights have no production caller until 18-06 and 18-10; they are referenced by the `switch` of both refusal functions and by this plan's tests, so nothing is unused.
- The first `/autoapprove` write on a chat with no greetings row creates the whole greetings row with its defaults (`upsertGreetingSettings`); the golden pins the whole row and 18-06's parity test proves the page leaves the same row (C-09).
