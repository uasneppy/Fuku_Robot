## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** to set what the bot says in a group when an admin bans, kicks, mutes, unbans or unmutes someone, from that group's settings in the bot's private chat, **so that** the replies sound like my community, while a group that sets nothing sees no change.

After this plan the Messages page is held to every settings rule of v1.1: the 64-byte callbacks, the 3800-unit screens in 8 languages, strict field checks, no writes on read, no Staff Group, no content in logs.

<objective>
The page's safeguards (D-23, the v1.1 settings rules every page carries): the worst-case callback test, the length test in all 8 languages with the row shapes, the forged-press test, the reads-create-nothing test, the cost test, the log hygiene test and the Messages screens in `TestSettingsStaffPrivacy`. A production change is made only when one of these finds a defect, in `settings_messages.go`, and is recorded in the SUMMARY.

Purpose: the page is new; its limits must be measured, not assumed, before the closing plan.
Output: two test files, the privacy test extended, AGENTS.md's Testing families.
</objective>
