## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** accounts whose names look like spam flagged by AI, **so that** advertising accounts are screened when they join, with no message text leaving the server.

After this plan a spam-looking joiner is muted, with one notice, by the real join entry through one Claude call and one cached verdict, and every way the call can fail leaves a member alone. The cost guards, the baseline for established members, the watcher's cached read, the notice tail, the button and the page status are the next plans; the rule is not yet safe to enable for a real group on its own, and the phase ships as one release.
