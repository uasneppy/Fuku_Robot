## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** members' commands, late edits and, when I choose, every message removed automatically, **so that** the group stays clean and an edited message cannot slip spam in later.

After this plan command deletion handles every way a command arrives (anonymous admins after their proof, refused and failed commands, held members, a Staff Group), no handler can reply after its command is gone, and a group where the bot lacks the delete right costs one check instead of a failing call per message for all three member rules.

<objective>
Widen the tracer of plan 17-03 to the anonymous-admin re-entry first (a real end-to-end slice through the proof tap), then to every sender and outcome, add the reply-ordering guard of C-03, and generalise the Phase 16 delete-right latch from service kinds to every kind that needs the right (D-02, D-17, D-22, D-23, D-24; research C-02, C-03, C-04, C-08).
Purpose: these are the edges where command deletion would otherwise delete at the wrong time, delete in a Staff Group, or hammer a rightless group forever; the latch change also serves edits and self-destruct, which arrive in plans 17-05 to 17-07.
Output: small production changes in four files, three test files, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
