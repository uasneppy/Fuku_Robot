## Planner assumptions (flagged)

- The bot-admin cache key is `fuku:memberreq:ba:<channel>` with a 2 minute TTL, positive only (CONTEXT leaves the key name and "short time" to the planner, D-22 and D-23). Two minutes bounds how long a demoted bot could still be read as an administrator for the breach gate (and the page's status line, which the channel screen refreshes on every open); the gate then costs at most one extra paced call per channel per two minutes, not one per breach.
- The D-22 gate also covers "restricted without is_member": that is a not-subscribed answer of the same kind as left, and the safe choice is to gate it identically (research S-06 table, flagged here because D-22 names only left and kicked).
- `memberReqChannelRule` reads the linked channel first and judges "no linked channel" as inert before any member call, so a group whose channel was unlinked makes no `getChatMember` request (D-05).
- A non-negative `linked_chat_id` is read as none, and a nil `getChat` answer is unknown: neither is cached. Telegram's `linked_chat_id` for an unlinked group is absent, which gotgbot decodes as 0 (research A5); spike S3 (does a plain-member bot get the field) is deferred, and its worst case is an inert channel rule in a group the bot cannot restrict in anyway.
- `Fresh` bypasses `lc`, `sub` and `ba` on read and refreshes them on a definite answer; on a definite breach it deletes a stale `sub` positive, and on a definite not-administrator it deletes a stale `ba` positive (so a removed subscription or a demoted bot is not hidden by a cache).
- The classifier is the one place an error is inspected, and only the bot's own `getChatMember(channel, bot)` error is. R3 is binding: `cache.IsBotGoneError` matches any Telegram 400 or 403, which errs only toward inert (nobody punished); spike S2 may narrow it.
- The functions of this plan have no production caller until plan 14-05 puts the channel rule in the table and plan 14-06 folds it into the executor; each is called by a test of this plan, and the SUMMARY says so (the brief's rule against an unused helper).
- Execution rules are `executor-brief-13.md`'s; read "of 14" in its STATE.md rule as "of 12". Test hygiene is plan 14-03's (fresh IDs, `mreqLookupCleanup`, the channel ID registered as a chat ID for `memberReqCleanup`, `restoreMemberReqRunner` after `StopMemberReqJobs`, the pacer restored by `withFastLookupPacer`, no `t.Parallel` with `withMemberReqLive`).
