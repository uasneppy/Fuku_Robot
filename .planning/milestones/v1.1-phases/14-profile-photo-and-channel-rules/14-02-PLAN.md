## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** require a profile photo and, in a channel's comment group, a subscription to that channel, **so that** throwaway accounts and outsiders are screened too, without anyone punished because a lookup failed.

After this plan every string the phase shows exists in the eight languages, fits Telegram and its own limits, and no sentence tells a member who only broke the channel rule to fix their profile.

<objective>
Add the 16 new and re-translate the 7 reworded locale keys of the approved UI contract in all 8 locale files, and move every test constant and test helper of `fuku/i18n` they affect to the new truth (D-16, D-01, D-15; UI-SPEC amendments M6, M10, M12 and planner notes R1, R4).

Purpose: the locale plan comes second so that no later plan leaves a real-locale test red or a literal `GetString` key missing: the rule table, the notices and the page (plans 14-05 to 14-10) find their words already in place.
Output: 23 keys in 8 files and two test files.
</objective>
