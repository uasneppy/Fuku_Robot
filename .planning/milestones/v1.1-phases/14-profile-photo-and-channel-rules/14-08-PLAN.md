## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** require a profile photo and, in a channel's comment group, a subscription to that channel, **so that** throwaway accounts and outsiders are screened too, without anyone punished because a lookup failed.

After this plan a member told "no visible profile photo" learns the real remedy, can fix it and tap "I fixed it" to be freed after a fresh live check, an approved member is freed at the tap, and a member who is still missing something is told exactly what.

<objective>
Add the photo remedy tail and the count-aware still-broken answer to the notice code, and extend "I fixed it" with step 4b (approvals), the fresh lookups of step 5 under a 5 s sub-budget, the broken-first answer and the channel inert classification, with the named tests that prove each branch (D-16, D-17, D-12, D-23, D-01; UI-SPEC amendments M10, M11, M12, M13, section 7, checks 10 and 11; research S-01, S-07, S-12).
Purpose: this completes the member-facing half of the profile-photo rule (MREQ-11): the notice says what to do, the button honours it, and nothing frees or keeps a member on a guess.
Output: notice and fixed-button code, three test files, AGENTS.md.
</objective>

## Planner assumptions (flagged)

- Approvals at the tap (M11, D-23): the existing `memberReqApprovedLookup` seam (`approvals.IsUserApprovedContext`, the cached list) is used, not a fresh read: the Redis layer is invalidated on approve and only another replica's in-process layer (`CACHE_LOCAL_TTL`, 10 s) can lag, so a member told "still broken" for up to 10 s taps again. Alternative for the owner: `approvals.ListApprovedIDsFresh` (no context, returns an error), at the price of a database read per press.
- Which answer wins when something is broken and another lookup is unknown (D-23, research Open Question 6): broken first, because it tells the member something true and actionable even when another lookup was shed; with nothing broken an unknown is `mreq_fixed_check_failed` and nothing changes.
- The press runs under `memberReqFixedTimeout` (10 s). Phase 13's own calls are the live `getChatMember`, `getChat` for the permissions, `restrictChatMember` for the lift and the notice edit; this plan adds at most four sequential paced lookup calls (photo, the linked channel fresh, the member in the channel, and the bot's own status only when the member's lookup was unanswerable) inside a 5 s sub-budget (Conflict C9), so at least 5 s remain for the unmute; a lookup that uses its 5 s is unknown and the answer is `mreq_fixed_check_failed`. The planner keeps the lookups sequential (no new goroutines in the press); a press needs no extra panic recovery beyond the callback's own.
- The notice tail is part of the text that plan 14-02's 520 cap renders; this plan appends it exactly as that test mirrors it, and `TestMemberReqScreensFitEveryLocale`, which already builds `allRules` from the table, picks the tail up with no edit beyond the count-aware answers below.
- Research Conflict C7: the UI contract says the Phase 13 tests `TestMemberReqFixedUnmutes` and `TestMemberReqFixedRefusals` use a member who is not approved. They approve nobody today, so they should need no edit; Task 3's second command runs both unchanged, and an edit is made only if one fails, named in the SUMMARY under deviations.
- The worst case of a member stuck behind an unknown at the tap (a requirement mute placed while the bot could read the channel, every later lookup unknown, no admin acting) keeps the 330-day marker mute until an admin frees them with `/unmute` or they rejoin (UI-SPEC P14-A9, accepted).
- Execution rules are `executor-brief-13.md`'s; read "of 14" in its STATE.md rule as "of 12". Test hygiene is plan 14-03's; the held-welcome subtests also delete the chat's `greeted_members` rows; any test that sends and joins takes dates before the message, at least 2 s apart; multi-goroutine tests use Mute or Kick, never Warn.
