## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** require a profile photo and, in a channel's comment group, a subscription to that channel, **so that** throwaway accounts and outsiders are screened too, without anyone punished because a lookup failed.

After this plan the executor enforces both rules: a member with no photo, or not subscribed to the linked channel, is warned, removed or muted through the Phase 13 punisher, once, with one notice, and nobody is punished when a lookup cannot be answered.

Purpose: this is the step where the new rules start to matter at runtime, so every way it can punish wrongly (an unknown, a paused chat, a shed, a cut-off) is proved to punish nobody.
Output: step 6b in `memberreq_enforce.go`, executor and shutdown tests, AGENTS.md.
</objective>

## Planner assumptions (flagged)

- Decision of research S-02, recorded here for the owner (research Open Question 4): an unknown lookup never blocks an established name breach. The alternative, aborting the whole episode on any unknown, would couple Phase 13's name rules to the lookup pacer's health: during a raid the pacer sheds and every name violator would escape. The cost is a partial episode (a Ban chosen for the photo rule is not applied to a member who was warned for a name rule while the photo lookup was shed); the hold's mask lacks the photo bit, and the member is re-judged for it after the hold clears.
- The budgets are research A8: 20 s for a job (the job context is 90 s and ends 30 s before `memberreq.StaleClaim`; the lookups run before the claim, so they cannot break that margin, but a worst case of three paced calls at 5 s of pacer wait plus 10 s of call each could eat 45 s and leave a mute notice short of time), 4 s for the join entry (its context is 15 s and it runs on a dispatcher goroutine; a raid of joins must not park 200 goroutines for 15 s each). Too tight only means more unknowns, never a punishment.
- The join entry needs no code change: `memberReqJoinThenWelcome` reads `memberreq.AnyRuleOn(settings)`, which iterates `memberreq.Rules` (plan 14-01), so a group with only the photo rule on is screened at join when Check on join is on, and the entry reaches step 6b through `memberReqEnforce` with `Source: memberReqFromJoin`.
- The watcher does not start a job for a member whose names are clean until plan 14-07, so these tests drive the executor directly (`mreqEnforce`) and through the join entry; the real-dispatcher end-to-end tests of the lookup rules are plan 14-07's.
- The Phase 13 held-path guarantee (a hold read before any live call) is re-asserted here only for a hold whose rules are name bits, which stays true after plan 14-07's held release; holds with a lookup bit are plan 14-07's.
- Multi-goroutine tests use Mute or Kick, never Warn, because on SQLite concurrent warn writers fail with "database is locked" where PostgreSQL waits (the lesson of `TestMemberReqOnePunishmentAcrossReplicas`). Any test that joins, rejoins or sends with a date takes the date before the message and uses `mreqSendAt` with dates at least 2 s apart (the second-boundary flake of commit 96b2f73: the join reset deletes holds created before the join's date, which has one-second resolution).
- Execution rules are `executor-brief-13.md`'s; read "of 14" in its STATE.md rule as "of 12". Test hygiene is plan 14-03's (fresh IDs, `memberReqCleanup` with the channel registered as a chat ID, `mreqLookupCleanup`, `restoreMemberReqRunner` after `StopMemberReqJobs`, the fast pacers, no `t.Parallel` with `withMemberReqLive`).
