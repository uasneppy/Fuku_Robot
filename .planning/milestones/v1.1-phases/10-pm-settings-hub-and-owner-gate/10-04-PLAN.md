## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want to** open my groups' settings from the bot's private chat and decide whether my admins may change them, **so that** I control who configures the bot.

This plan prepares the test environment the gate, the Access page, the feed and the list are proven in, wires plan 10-01's PostgreSQL guarantees into CI, and writes the rules for the new tables and the 8 locales into AGENTS.md. The harness seeding is the single biggest planning risk named by the research (R-09). Split out of the original plan 10-01 during revision so that plan stays within its file budget.
