## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** Telegram's service messages and the bot's own messages removed on a schedule I set, **so that** the chat stays readable.

After this plan the owner sets every service-message type and every kind of the bot's own message from a Deletion page of the settings menu, in the group or in private, sees at once whether the bot can delete there, and the page does not exist inside a Staff Group.

<objective>
Add the Deletion page (hub, Service messages and Bot messages lists, one screen per row, preset choices through the apply functions), the delete-right line, the Staff Group exclusion with the one `Hidden` framework field, and the tests that bind them (D-03, D-04, D-11, D-14 to D-18, D-28 and M24 to M28; research S-06; UI-SPEC checks 1 to 16 minus the privacy sweep that plan 16-09 owns). D-28 (amending D-15) is the one framework change of the plan: `settings_pages.go` and `settings_menu.go` are edited only for the optional `Hidden` field, its skip in `settingsMainEntries` and its guard in `openPage`, and for nothing else.

Purpose: this is where DEL-01 and DEL-02 become something an owner can press and where DEL-08 and the third layer of DEL-07 live; rows, watcher, worker and hooks are already in place.
Output: one new page file, two small framework edits, three test files, the three edited Main-shape tests, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
