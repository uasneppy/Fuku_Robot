## Phase Goal

**As a** group owner, **I want** Telegram's service messages and the bot's own messages removed on a schedule I set, **so that** the chat stays readable.

After this plan the member requirement notices (MREQ-15) and the other automatic punishment notices (antiflood, blacklist, the AI spam breaker's pause notice) are queued for deletion by the automatic-punishments timer, with the mute notice held up to a day for the member's "I fixed it" button and rescheduled once they tap it.

Add the two variants of the shared scheduling body (the 24 hour cap and the reschedule from now), hook `memberReqPostNotice`, the "I fixed it" edit, the antiflood notice, the blacklist's mute, ban and kick notices and the AI spam breaker notice (D-11, D-12, D-14, D-25, D-27, MREQ-15; research S-04 "Automatic punishment notices", C-07).

Purpose: the Bot messages page of plan 16-08 offers Auto punishments as a kind; this plan makes every automatic notice obey it, which completes the bot-message hooks.
Output: two small helpers, six edited production files, two test files, AGENTS.md.
</objective>
