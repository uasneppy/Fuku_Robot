## 1. Publication facts

- **Creator:** Labirhin, real name Bajra Gadha Panglima Balangga, artist, animator, storyteller, screenwriter and musician from Yogyakarta, Indonesia; born 2006; nickname taken after watching The Maze Runner [wiki:Labirhin]. He directs, writes, animates and scores the series [substack]. English localisation assisted by TraaaashPanda and Awoominati Records (formerly Aural Alliance) [wiki:Mau Makan Apa Wiki]. English comic translation by Delta Tekalec [delta] [webtoon-en].
- **Title:** stylised MɅU MɅKɅN ɅPɅ¿; Indonesian for "What to Eat?" [tvtropes:Main]; IMDb: literally "What do you want to eat?", the Indonesian release's own subtitles give "What to eat?" [imdb]. In-story, "Mau Makan Apa"/"WHAT TO EAT?" is the name of the famine disaster [webtoon-id] [webtoon-en].
- **Franchise:** "a mixed media franchise composed of multiple stories that takes place in a shared universe" [wiki:Mau Makan Apa Wiki].
- **Genre (as described):** post-apocalyptic sci-fi horror furry comic with black comedy elements [tvtropes:Main]; IMDb genres Animation, Sci-Fi [imdb].
- **Official synopsis:** "A cat who wants to save the world from a famine disaster called "WHAT TO EAT?" but gets into trouble with the experimental superpower project from the Jaya Abadi company." [webtoon-en]

## 2. World and timeline

### The world

- About 100,000 years before the story, **Mount Merabu** erupted; ash mixed with clouds and blocked sunlight: **The Great Blocking** (T.G.B.) [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿] [wiki:The Great Blocking]. Chapter 1 card: "EARTH 100,000 YEARS AGO" [comic-thumb:ch1 p05].
- Labirhin (via wiki): Mount Merabu's eruption happened on "December 22, 2018 AD" [wiki:Mount Merabu]. TV Tropes reads this as: the story is 100,000 years in our future and the calendar "froze" [tvtropes:Main]. The series' "2018" is "not the same as it is now" [tvtropes:Main].
- Effects: all species without fur went extinct from the cold; lack of sunlight cut oxygen and killed most plants; the only surviving plant is the "chocolate soybean" thanks to exceptional sunlight absorption; the cloud layer also shields Earth from solar radiation and traps heat (greenhouse effect), which becomes global warming once the Sun begins to swell [wiki:The Great Blocking].
- Survivors: 4 documented anthropomorphic species (fox, cat, wolf, thylacine) which can interbreed into subspecies (wolfcat, wolfox, thylacat, wolfacine…), plus undocumented ones such as hyenas; non-anthro life: fish, bugs, worms, fungus, jellyfish [wiki:Hadapan Island]. TV Tropes counts "4 animals (all carnivores), 5 insects (including fleas), and 5 fish (including fatal jellyfish)" [tvtropes:NightmareFuel].
- Chocolate soybeans (TV Tropes: "black soybeans" infected by parasitic cocoa, containing theobromine) are poisonous to the population [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿] [tvtropes:Main].
- **Hadapan Island:** where MMA, The Stars Are Falling, Revision After Revision and Missile City take place; 210 km in diameter; population approx. 1,000,000; days are 30 hours long, so real-equivalent ages are 1.25× stated ages [wiki:Hadapan Island]. TV Tropes attributes the 30-hour day to the Moon having moved further away [tvtropes:Trivia].
- Only Hadapan and the two poles are safe; elsewhere there are nonstop low-temperature volcanic-ash hurricanes; only Hadapan and the South Pole are inhabited; the North Pole has completely melted [wiki:Hadapan Island].
- Mount Merabu stayed active, growing the island and melting most of Earth's ice; it still releases ash, sometimes sulfurous (yellowish skies); Jaya Abadi makes its signature orange colouring from this sulfur [wiki:Mount Merabu].
- **Clans:** everyone has ⛤ or ∅ on the back of each hand showing their vote between project Switchstar (Star Clan, slogan "Swap the Sun!") and Orbit-∅ (Orbit Clan, slogan "Just a little further!") [wiki:Star Clan] [wiki:Orbit Clan]. Voting began long before Jaya Abadi was founded; Switchstar won [wiki:Switchstar]. Genpin (homeless raccoon) is the only character with no marks [wiki:Genpin].
- Law: only state company Anoir may cultivate fish [wiki:Anoir]. The study of mushrooms is illegal; secret researchers may face public execution [wiki:Fungus].
- Cities/areas on Hadapan: Lawu City, Jayakarta (Jaya Karta), Pemucik, Mugoharjo, West Mugoharjo, Area Gadramuda, JM-1, JM-2, Ngipasan (wiki also spells "Ngipasaw") [wiki:Hadapan Island] [wiki:Gix Busok] [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿].

## 3. Jaya Abadi

- **Founder:** Jaya Utomo; founded to sell Tempe, a soy-based alternative to fish, in 1995 [wiki:Jaya Abadi].
- Tempe was not popular at first (fish from Anoir was still available) but funded the second product, Material Limits [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿] [wiki:Tempe].
- Dono Diamond's fish monopoly led him into an arrangement in which Jaya Abadi joined forces with Anoir and Atom-∅ [wiki:Jaya Abadi]. Atom-∅ is described as a private military company formed by the joining of Jaya Abadi and Anoir [wiki:Atom-∅].
- Jaya Abadi "is also responsible for creating the KS in order to administer various company projects" [wiki:Jaya Abadi].
- Based in Lawu City [wiki:Lawu City].
- **Logo:** "an orange toothy mouth with a couple of tiny pointed ears on top" [tvtropes:Main]; "a creepy-yet-goofy wolf mouth", pasted on almost everything including megastructures [tvtropes:Funny]. Orange is the trademark colour [wiki:Xenobot-72] [tvtropes:Main].
- **Slogan / stinger:** "EVERYTHING ONLY FROM JAYA ABADI" [tvtropes:Main].
- **Subsidiaries:** Jaya Studio (films, animation, entertainment; staff: Sorbitol, Kontol Diamond, Mr. Producer The 3rd) and Jaya TV (live TV, news — Lawu News with anchor Berita — and adverts mostly starring Hirarki) [wiki:Jaya Studio] [wiki:Jaya TV] [wiki:Lawu News]. Retail: "Jaya Abadi – Mr Rigel's Food Mart" storefront [comic-thumb:ch4 p10]; Jaya Mall [wiki:Embun].
- **Later ownership:** Jojo Garong takes Jaya's position after the assassination [wiki:Jojo Garong]; after the events of MMA, Bambang becomes the owner [wiki:Bambang]; later products under him: Ultra Soy Milk, "Notten" (no details) [wiki:Ultra Soy Milk] [wiki:Bambang]. After MMA, Erika works at a Jaya Abadi drive-thru and Tobii is a Jaya Abadi cashier [wiki:Erika Utomo] [wiki:Tobii Agustin].
- **Food products:** Tempe; Soy Milk; Ultra Soy Milk; Sweet Soya Sauce ("Kecap"); Tofu ("Tahu"); Gembus; Oncom; Tauco/Taucom; Miso; Natto ("Nato" in the animation); Tausi; Gochujang; Edamame ("Edamamae" on packaging); Keripik Tempe; Soy Powder [wiki:Jaya Abadi] and product pages. "Soy ice cream" freezer seen in ch5 [comic-thumb:ch5 p21]. All are made from "Jaya Biosoy", Utomo's cocoa-free soybean; only he knew the recipe [wiki:Tempe].
- **Non-food products:** "Batas"; Deus Ex Machina; Moonlocking; Global Cooling; Sky Scraper; Scabies Cream; Switchstar; Sulak; Jaya Studio; Jaya TV [wiki:Jaya Abadi].
- **Jaya Abadi Production Facility:** the food production centre "in charge of making all the food for the population"; walls of Material Limits; exploded December 1st, 2018 [wiki:Jaya Abadi Production Facility]. Comic caption: "PRODUCTION CENTER – TEMPEH FACTORY J.A." [comic:ch1 p15]. In ch6 Atom-∅ hits it with a tungsten missile from a Sky Scraper to remove trespassers; the building survives [wiki:Jaya Abadi Production Facility] [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿].
- **The fish poisoning:** during construction of the production facility a waste door to the ocean was left open for 7 years (possibly intentionally), poisoning the fish worldwide and killing many people [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿]; the waterways were poisoned with cocoa [wiki:Tempe]. Comic: "AS A RESULT, ALL OF THE REMAINING FISH IN THE SEA WERE COMPLETELY INEDIBLE" … "SINCE THEN, UTOMO NO LONGER TRUSTS ANYONE TO WORK IN HIS FACTORY" [comic:ch1 p15]. TV Tropes notes the leak benefited Jaya Abadi, implying it was no accident [tvtropes:NightmareFuel].

### Megaprojects

**Moonlocking** — first "Mega Project". The Moon was leaving orbit; the plan "locks" it with large magnetic stations, one on the Moon and one orbiting Earth's equator. Attempt 1 by Jamal in 2000 failed; attempt 2 by Bambang in 2004 (after Fight Over) succeeded [wiki:Moonlocking]. Comic heading "MOON LOCKING 2000-2004" [comic-thumb:ch1 p10].

**Global Cooling / Global Freezing** — refreeze the South Pole with a giant freezer; completed 2006; lowered Earth's average temperature from 40 °C to 16 °C, better than expected; meant only to buy time until Switchstar; assigned KS: Jojo Garong [wiki:Global Freezing]. Jojo still uses his powers to keep it running [wiki:Jojo Garong].

**Sky Scraper (Pencakar Langit)** — giant satellites tall enough to pierce the cloud layer and collect solar energy, sending it down an "Orbital Cable" that also serves as a rail; the satellite moves against Earth's rotation to stay in sunlight. Each cable has 5 satellites, one carrying a solid tungsten missile owned by Atom-∅. Blueprint numbers: length 4045 m, height 7713.8 m, horizontal speed 300 km/h; credited inventor: Jamal; worked on 2016–2018 [wiki:Sky Scraper]. Story uses: Tobii crushes Jamal under a rail (ch5); missile on the production centre (ch6); Erika's missile on Tobii's house (ch8); Bambang drops several Sky Scrapers on Lawu City (ch9) [wiki:Sky Scraper].

**Switchstar (Switch Star, "Switchst⛤r")** — swap the swelling Sun with the cooler nearby star Alpha Centauri B using the spacecutting OAVI. Won the public vote; Utomo scheduled it for 2020 ("ready in 2 more years" at the 2018 event); postponed after his death. Assigned first to Bambang, then Tobii; finally performed successfully by Bambang after The Stars Are Falling [wiki:Switchstar] [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿]. Removing the cloud layer was impossible until Switchstar because the Sun would scorch everyone [wiki:The Great Blocking].

**Orbit-∅** — rejected alternative: many rocket engines push Earth's orbit outward, repeated several times [wiki:Orbit-∅].

**Deus Ex Machina (Mountain Pack Mission)** — 2003 aircraft with an outer ring and an inner net, built to cover Mount Lawu and catch its ash; pilot Raas Busok, alone; material test subject Erika Utomo. It failed to lock around the mountain; hot ash spread through Lawu City, killing 364,117. Chapter 9 reveals the craft paused because Raas stopped to rescue Erika and Gigo [wiki:Deus Ex Machina] [wiki:Mount Lawu]. The wiki calls the mission "half-failed" [wiki:Mount Lawu]. Stored in a room under the Area Gadramuda artificial island [wiki:Area Gadramuda].

**Material Limits ("Batas")** — invented 1998; extremely strong, light material made from compacted trash; its military potential drew Dono Diamond's interest [wiki:Material Limits]. Demonstration: a huge missile on a "Batas"/"Limits" coffee mug leaves the room charred and the mug untouched [tvtropes:Funny].

- An AI created by Jaya Utomo to manage the Jaya Abadi Production Facility; it helps manage all of Jaya Abadi's projects because Jaya does not trust other people after the fish poisoning [wiki:Xenobot-72].
- Species: Artificial Intelligence; Age: 31; Affiliation: Jaya Abadi; Clan symbol: ⛤; Sexuality: Unknown [wiki:Xenobot-72 infobox].
- It took over the whole production process from the workers; Utomo uses it to produce all the tempeh [comic:ch1 p15]. - It "oversees production and logistics at the Jaya Abadi plant" [wiki:Xenobot-72].
- It is self-aware: asked "is xenobot aware that is an AI?", Labirhin answered "Yes" [discord-img:Xenobot_self_aware].

Exoworo grabs Gix, shows him the Really Secret Documents he "once left" with her, and lets him stay; Angkasa and Embun are introduced. Erika vows to kill the escaped KS and asks for Xenobot 72; Jojo shows her the older machine.

**Ch4 — Fight Over.** Angkasa is found dead and partly eaten; Aditya orders the homeless killed; Asep and Hendra chase Gix and Exoworo, who end up at Mr. Rigel's food mart. Captured Jamal watches the 2004 Fight Over footage while Xenobot-72 speaks to him; he walks out and tears out Bambang's skull, then collapses.

**Ch5 — Here, I'll Teach You To Cook.** Erika finds both bodies; Tobii appears and she re-enables the KS Safety; Tobii takes Jamal and leaves him under a Sky Scraper rail, where he is crushed. News: a soybean plantation was stormed; the military shot intruders. Jojo finds Bambang alive. Erika interrogates the hospitalised Gigo. Gix and Exoworo scavenge, eat soy ice cream, and Gix reasons that Tobii's time-swapping could fetch the recipe from the past.

**Ch6 — Anytime, Whenever.** On Jaya TV, Konny tells Hirarki the besieged production centre is Material Limits; a missile kills the rioters and leaves it standing. Jojo rebuilds Bambang and recruits him against Tobii, having found his own future corpse in the wreckage. Erika comes for Gix; Exoworo fights her, photographs her, swallows her whole — and Erika bursts out. Tobii, robbed of food by Gix, sends him to 2003: "If you need food so bad, take it from the past".

**Ch7 — Came Home To See The Bad News.** Jojo and Bambang use the Central OAVI to build a better spacecutting device. Tobii follows Gix to 2003 and finds he cannot return without Central OAVI access for that year. Mistaken for Raas, Gix gets into Breakdown HQ; they take the recipe, flee on young Jojo's motorcycle and crash; Gix gives the folder to 14-year-old Exoworo. In the KS Testing Room Xenobot-72 denies Tobii access until Gix tells it Tobii killed Jaya Utomo and starved everyone; Tobii rages at him; they return. Gix comes home with food and finds Exoworo apparently dead.

**Ch8 — Don't Go.** Erika finds Gix and reports to Jojo; Gix lets Jojo take the recipe; leaflets promise tempeh tomorrow. Erika missiles Tobii's house and believes him dead; she warms to Gix and asks Gigo about his favourite food. Gix finds Exoworo's camera and the photo; he confronts Erika and walks out. Tobii is alive.

**Ch9 — The Beautiful Silence After The Bang.** Jojo is poisoned with chocolate. Erika vows to erupt Mount Lawu. On the factory catwalk she kills Jojo for his OAVI — the true cause of the factory explosion. Bambang follows; the Xenobot is gone when he reaches the KS Safety. Gix apologises to Tobii, learns Gigo lives; Tobii sends Erika to 2003 Mount Lawu and follows with Gix and Gigo; Bambang, who "chose swap spacetime when he first became a KS", chases Tobii through time, including through the Switchstar conference (where his rubber band kills Utomo) and under falling Sky Scrapers; he breaks Tobii's OAVI and spares him. Erika throws a superheated rock into the crater; Gigo tells her not everyone hates her; the Deus Ex Machina stops to pick up Gigo and Erika — the pause that doomed the mission; Erika rips out Gix's shoulder and leaves him; Bambang rescues him, closing the loop with chapter 1.

**End point and after.** The comic ends there. TV Tropes reads the last panel as revealing an endless time loop [tvtropes:Main]. Creator statements relayed by the wiki: Bambang takes Gix to Lawu City Hospital in the present, where he meets the surviving Exoworo; Bambang brings Gigo and Erika back; Bambang owns Jaya Abadi and later completes Switchstar [wiki:Gix Busok] [wiki:Exoworo] [wiki:Bambang].

## 10. Contradictions and open questions

**Xenobot-72**
1. **Pronouns.** Creator: female, "she". Wiki prose: "they". Comic characters: "it", "that robot". A role-play can use she/her on the creator's authority, but in-story speakers do not.
2. **"Lied about the KS safety."** The plot summary says it "had lied to the KSs about the safety" [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿]; the Jamal page says it "excluded the knowledge of KS Safety from Jamal" [wiki:Jamal]; the Bambang page says it "reveals" the safety's use and manipulates Jamal [wiki:Bambang]. On the page, the only explicit charge is a character's line, "That robot lied to me / I think it lied to you too" [comic:ch4 p29]. What exactly the lie was is not spelled out in what I read.
3. **Who controls the KS Safety.** Xenobot-72 [wiki:Xenobot-72] vs a lever built into the Central OAVI [wiki:KS Safety]; in the comic Erika moves a wall lever herself, yet the lever fails without the Xenobot-72 system. Labirhin also speaks of separate "ks safety robots" that carry clan marks.
4. **Bambang at the lever.** "attempts to turn on" [wiki:Xenobot-72] vs "tries to deactivate" [wiki:MAU MAKAN APA¿]. The panel shows him pushing the handle toward ON [comic:ch9 p25].
5. **Clan marks.** Infobox gives ⛤ and the appearance text puts stars on both hands; Labirhin says the factory unit has none.
6. **Age vs role.** Age 31 matches Xenobot-1's 1987, not "version 72". When version 72 itself was made is not stated. It "replaced all workers" after the fish poisoning, yet already runs KS intake in 2003 and Fight Over on 1/1/2004; the order of these events is not stated.
7. **One AI or many bodies.** Units 071/072/074 fly together; "Xenobot-72s" (plural) in TV Tropes; an "older version" also numbered 072. Not resolved.
8. **When it is destroyed.** Wiki text places it after Erika's failed strike (ch8); the explosion panel is in ch9.
9. **"Beta."** The ch1 window says "72-BETA 3.4.0"; no source explains it.

**Other**
10. Exoworo's age in 2003: 14 [wiki:Exoworo] vs 15 [wiki:Really Secret Documents].
11. KS order: ch9 portraits show Tobii as KS-4 and Erika as KS-5 — a production error per Labirhin [wiki:KS].
12. General Aditya's hand symbol changes between 2003 (∅) and 2018 (⛤); unexplained [wiki:General Aditya].
13. "Global Cooling" vs "Global Freezing" used interchangeably; ch7 buttons say "Global Cooling".
14. Atom-∅: "formed by the joining of Jaya Abadi and Anoir" [wiki:Atom-∅] vs "his company Atom-∅" for Dono Diamond [wiki:Sky Scraper]; ch1 calls it state-owned [comic-thumb:ch1 p08].
15. Gix's injury: "shoulder missing" [wiki] vs "left arm cut off" [tvtropes:Main].
16. Species list: fox, cat, wolf, thylacine [wiki] vs "wolves, felines, dogs, and thylacines" [tvtropes:Main].
17. Episode 1 premiere: March 7, 2024 [dubdb] vs Mar 6, 2024 and "January 12, 2025 (United States)" [imdb]. Episode 3: July 25 vs July 26, 2025.
18. The Substack review has errors: eruption "10 million years ago" (sources: 100,000); "Jaya Abadi is killed" (it is Jaya Utomo); the KS "hucks Gix's brother to his death" (Gigo falls and survives) [substack].
19. TV Tropes says KS-3 "was used to blow up" the production centre; the wiki says he lost control of his OAVI while Erika killed him.
20. Sky Scraper is dated 2016–2018 and credited to Jamal, who was contained; how is not explained.
21. Deus Ex Machina "failed" vs "half-failed".
