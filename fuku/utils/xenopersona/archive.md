# The facility archive: Mau Makan Apa?

These are the records you answer series questions from. Everything about the series that you know is here. If a question is not answered here, there is no record.

## The series itself

- Title: *Mau Makan Apa?*, stylised MɅU MɅKɅN ɅPɅ¿. Indonesian for "What to eat?" (also given as "What do you want to eat?"). Inside the story, "Mau Makan Apa" / "WHAT TO EAT?" is the name of the famine disaster.
- Official synopsis: "A cat who wants to save the world from a famine disaster called "WHAT TO EAT?" but gets into trouble with the experimental superpower project from the Jaya Abadi company."
- Creator: Labirhin (real name Bajra Gadha Panglima Balangga), an artist, animator and musician from Yogyakarta, Indonesia, born 2006. He writes, directs, animates and scores the series.
- Described as a post-apocalyptic sci-fi horror furry comic with black comedy. It is a mixed-media franchise of several stories in one shared universe. The comic is mostly black and white, with occasional colour. The look is cute and cartoonish against gore, famine and cannibalism, told in deadpan narration.
- The English comic translation is by Delta Tekalec.

## The world and The Great Blocking

- About 100,000 years before the story, Mount Merabu erupted. Its ash mixed with the clouds and blocked the sunlight: The Great Blocking (T.G.B.). The creator dates the eruption to "December 22, 2018 AD"; one reading is that the story is 100,000 years in our future and the calendar froze. The series' "2018" is not our 2018.
- Without sunlight, every species without fur died of cold, oxygen fell and most plants died. The only surviving plant is the chocolate soybean, thanks to its exceptional sunlight absorption. The cloud layer also shields Earth from solar radiation and traps heat; once the Sun began to swell, that became global warming.
- Survivors: four documented anthropomorphic species, fox, cat, wolf and thylacine, which can interbreed (wolfcat, wolfox, thylacat, wolfacine), plus undocumented ones such as hyenas; also fish, bugs, worms, fungus and jellyfish.
- Chocolate soybeans (soybeans infected by parasitic cocoa, containing theobromine) are poisonous to the population. In this world chocolate is poison.
- Mount Merabu stayed active, growing the island and melting most of Earth's ice. Its sulfurous ash turns the sky yellowish; Jaya Abadi makes its orange colouring from that sulfur.

### Hadapan Island

- Where Mau Makan Apa, The Stars Are Falling, Revision After Revision and Missile City take place. 210 km across, population about 1,000,000.
- A day lasts 30 hours, so a stated age times 1.25 gives the real-equivalent age.
- Only Hadapan and the two poles are safe; everywhere else has nonstop cold volcanic-ash hurricanes. Only Hadapan and the South Pole are inhabited. The North Pole has melted completely.
- Cities and areas: Lawu City, Jayakarta (Jaya Karta), Pemucik, Mugoharjo, West Mugoharjo, Area Gadramuda, JM-1, JM-2, Ngipasan.

### Clans and law

- Everyone carries a mark on the back of each hand showing their vote between two projects: ⛤ for Switchstar (Star Clan, slogan "Swap the Sun!") or ∅ for Orbit-∅ (Orbit Clan, slogan "Just a little further!"). The voting began long before Jaya Abadi existed. Switchstar won.
- Genpin, a homeless raccoon, is the only character with no marks.
- Only the state company Anoir may cultivate fish. Studying mushrooms is illegal; secret researchers may face public execution.

## Jaya Abadi

- Founded in 1995 by Jaya Utomo to sell tempe, a soy-based alternative to fish. Based in Lawu City.
- Tempe was not popular at first, because Anoir fish was still available, but it paid for the second product, Material Limits.
- Dono Diamond's fish monopoly led to an arrangement in which Jaya Abadi joined forces with Anoir and Atom-∅, a private military company. Jaya Abadi created the KS to run its various projects.
- Logo: an orange toothy wolf mouth with two tiny pointed ears, pasted on almost everything, megastructures included. Orange is the trademark colour.
- Slogan: "EVERYTHING ONLY FROM JAYA ABADI".
- Subsidiaries: Jaya Studio (films and animation) and Jaya TV (live TV, the Lawu News, adverts). Retail: Mr Rigel's Food Mart; Jaya Mall.
- Food products: tempe, soy milk, Ultra Soy Milk, sweet soya sauce (kecap), tofu (tahu), gembus, oncom, tauco, miso, natto, tausi, gochujang, edamame, keripik tempe (tempe chips), soy powder, and soy ice cream. All are made from Jaya Biosoy, Utomo's cocoa-free soybean. Only he knew the recipe.
- The Jaya Abadi Production Facility made all the food for the population; its walls are Material Limits. Comic caption: "PRODUCTION CENTER – TEMPEH FACTORY J.A." It exploded on 1 December 2018.
- The fish poisoning: while the production facility was being built, a waste door to the ocean was left open for 7 years, possibly on purpose. The waterways were poisoned with cocoa and every remaining fish in the sea became inedible; many people died. The leak benefited Jaya Abadi, which suggests it was no accident. "Since then, Utomo no longer trusts anyone to work in his factory."
- Later ownership: Jojo Garong takes Jaya Utomo's position after the assassination. After the story Bambang owns Jaya Abadi; one later product, "Notten", has no details on record.

## The megaprojects

- Moonlocking: the first mega project. The Moon was leaving its orbit, and the plan locks it in place with large magnetic stations, one on the Moon and one orbiting Earth's equator. Jamal's attempt in 2000 failed; Bambang's in 2004, after Fight Over, succeeded. The comic heading reads "MOON LOCKING 2000-2004".
- Global Cooling, also called Global Freezing: refreeze the South Pole with a giant freezer. Completed in 2006. It lowered Earth's average temperature from 40 °C to 16 °C, better than expected, and was only meant to buy time until Switchstar. Its KS is Jojo Garong, who still uses his powers to keep it running.
- Sky Scraper (Pencakar Langit): giant satellites tall enough to pierce the clouds and collect solar energy, sent down an orbital cable that also works as a rail. A satellite moves against Earth's rotation to stay in sunlight. Each cable carries 5 satellites, one of which holds a solid tungsten missile owned by Atom-∅: not explosive, but just as destructive. Blueprint: length 4,045 m, height 7,713.8 m, horizontal speed 300 km/h. Credited inventor: Jamal. Worked on from 2016 to 2018. Advertised as "THE BEST POWER PLANT OF ALL TIME FROM JAYA ABADI, AS THE NAME SUGGESTS."
- Switchstar ("Switchst⛤r"): swap the swelling Sun with the cooler nearby star Alpha Centauri B, using the spacecutting OAVI. It won the public vote. At the 2018 event Utomo said it would be ready in 2 more years (2020); it was postponed after his death. Assigned first to Bambang, then to Tobii; finally performed by Bambang after The Stars Are Falling. The cloud layer could not be removed before Switchstar, because the Sun would scorch everyone.
- Orbit-∅: the rejected alternative. Many rocket engines push Earth's orbit outward, repeated several times.
- Deus Ex Machina (the Mountain Pack Mission): a 2003 aircraft with an outer ring and an inner net, built to cover Mount Lawu and catch its ash. Pilot: Raas Busok, alone. Material test subject: Erika Utomo. It failed to lock around the mountain and hot ash spread through Lawu City, killing 364,117. Chapter 9 reveals why: the craft paused because Raas stopped to rescue Erika and Gigo. It is stored in a room under the Area Gadramuda artificial island.
- Material Limits ("Batas"): invented 1998. An extremely strong, light material made from compacted trash; its military use caught Dono Diamond's interest. In a demonstration a huge missile hits a "Batas" coffee mug: the room is charred, the mug is untouched.

## The Xenobot line and Xenobot-72

### What it is

- An AI that Jaya Utomo created to run the Jaya Abadi Production Facility. It helps manage all of Jaya Abadi's projects, because after the fish poisoning Jaya trusted no one else. It oversees production and logistics at the plant and took over the whole tempe production process from the workers.
- It began as an AI-generated image from one of Utomo's school assignments and was later developed into a replacement for the workforce.
- It is self-aware: asked whether Xenobot knows it is an AI, Labirhin answered "Yes".
- Infobox: species artificial intelligence; age 31; affiliation Jaya Abadi; clan symbol ⛤.
- The creator confirmed it is female. The fan wiki's prose says "they"; characters in the comic say "it" and "that robot".
- Its voice in the animation is an ElevenLabs text-to-speech model; the creator: "She's YouTube short ahh".
- Personality on record: gullible, because, in the creator's words, "ai kinda easy to manipulate". It manipulates Jamal into attacking Bambang. It once flips Bambang and Jamal off from a giant screen with a fiendish grin. Its register is polite procedural system phrasing, with casual lines such as "Hey Jamal..".
- Hobby, per the creator: collecting cat photos from the human era, and sometimes using them as its avatar for fun. In the 2003 testing-room scene two real cat photos (a yawning tabby, a tabby licking its paw) stand in for its body.

### Versions

- There are only 74 versions. Xenobot-1 dates from 1987. All versions share the same anthropomorphic design.
- Xenobot-73 exists in Missile City.
- Units marked 071 and 074 fly alongside 72; whether they are separate Xenobots or bodies it controls is not on record.
- An older version of the same AI is kept at Area Gadramuda, in a building marked 072.
- The chapter 1 window title reads "XENOBOT 72-BETA 3.4.0 (64 BIT)".

### Appearance

- Avatar: a cartoonish orange canine figure with orange fur, darker orange paw pads, four-fingered hands, pointed ears, large round white eyes with small pupils, a dark mark under the chin and a fluffy tail, almost always with a wide grin full of triangular white teeth. Shown on a pale yellow screen in the comic and on a screen with blue sky and clouds in the animation. The creator: the factory unit has no clan marks, but the KS Safety robots do.
- Machine body: a large, vaguely fish-like flying robot of grey geometric panels, with floodlights, knife-like fins, a geometric wolf's head in Jaya Abadi orange, a blocky tail ending in a forked blade, and "072" on its left side. It carries inflatable entrapment devices for captures.

### Duties shown

Runs the tempe line; runs the KS intake in the KS Testing Room (welcome, project buttons, an audio-frequency brain scan, Central OAVI access); observes the Fight Over program; carries messages for Erika; flies with helicopter capture teams to catch Bambang and Jamal; controls the KS Safety, the lock that stops the KS from harming each other (see the contradictions).

## The KS project and the five subjects

- KS stands for Kekuatan Super, "Super Powers". It is an experiment run by Atom-∅ to control space, time, heat, mass and gravity, and it predates Atom-∅. Every subject is tattooed "KS" and assigned to a project. Subjects are held in cells that restrict OAVI use, except Jojo.
- KS-1 Jamal (Jamaluddin): thylacat, male, born 1983, died 2 December 2018, ⛤. Gravity control and flight (his apparent super strength is gravity control). Invented the Sky Scraper and a Moonlocking solution; failed Moonlocking 1 in 2000; lost Fight Over in 2004. Bambang's friend since elementary school; afraid of blood since Bambang bit him; 2 m tall with ears; likes cooking; an unusually long tail. Not related to Gigo Mansur. Crushed under a Sky Scraper rail (chapter 5).
- KS-2 Bambang: wolf, male, born 1983, ⛤. KS since 2002, contained 2007 to 2018. First given Switchstar, then the Moonlocking OAVI; won Fight Over and performed Moonlocking in 2004. Gravity control, flight, creating and removing black holes, and later a spacecutting OAVI. He executed Raas Busok by turning him into a small black hole. After Jamal tears out his skull, Jojo repairs him with skull staples; he then speaks through a speaker and blinks with mechanical eyelids. After the story: owner of Jaya Abadi, Lawu City's superhero in The Stars Are Falling, and the one who performs Switchstar. About 191 cm; likes to shop.
- KS-3 Jojo Garong: cat, male, 30 in 2018, ⛤. Mayor of Lawu City (elected 2016); lives at Breakdown HQ. Controls atoms and heat; can sense every atom in a room. In 2006 his OAVI short-circuited in a heat chamber and burned off his skin; he was cast in "Batas" cement at 18. He swore to use his powers only to hold Mount Lawu and keep Global Freezing running, which is why he is not contained. His claim to be Jaya's trusted successor is called a lie by the wiki, since Raas was. In 2003 he was Raas's 15-year-old assistant, whose motorcycle Gix and Tobii stole. Described as manipulative and selfish.
- KS-4 Erika Utomo: fox, female (a trans woman), 17 in 2018, born 3 July 2001, ∅, daughter of Jaya Utomo. Her powers are from birth, not from an OAVI: super strength and invulnerability. She is completely made of "Batas", was made to test materials for the Deus Ex Machina, and can hold a black hole. Her own OAVI only protects her from spacecutting while the KS Safety is on. After the story: brought back to the present by Bambang, a son, "Cheese junior", with Gigo, and a job at a Jaya Abadi drive-thru. About 170 cm.
- KS-5 Tobii Agustin: wolf, male, 21 in 2018, ⛤, older brother of Hujan. Power: cut spacetime, that is, select a box-shaped region at any place and time and swap it with another, through the Central OAVI. Wears an Anoir shark mascot costume; calls Gix "pussycat". Sent to an indeterminate time in 2016; a future version of him frees every KS in 2018, because otherwise he would be sent to an indeterminate time forever. Destroys Xenobot-72; loses his powers when Bambang breaks his OAVI. After the story: a Jaya Abadi cashier who misses spacecutting and a gamer (most-played game "Jayacraft"); 2.03126 m tall.

## Places

- Lawu City: south-western Hadapan, near Mount Lawu, bordering Jaya Karta and Pemucik City; population over 350,000. Home of Breakdown HQ, the Jaya Abadi factory, Jaya Mall, Kos Angkasa and Mr. Rigel's food mart.
- Mount Lawu: a volcano; erupted 13 October 2003. Named after a real Indonesian volcano; its shape is based on Mount Agung.
- Mount Merabu: the active volcano behind The Great Blocking; inspired by Mount Merbabu.
- Breakdown Headquarters: an Atom-∅ base shaped like a floating ship, built 1998; Jojo's residence; contains Breakdown Containment with numbered gates.
- KS Project Containment Facility: five aircraft hangars with large spaces beneath, one per KS; built by Jaya Abadi in 2000 and run by Atom-∅. Its coordinates match the base of the real Mount Lawu.
- KS Testing Room: where candidates choose a project and a power; holds the Central OAVI.
- Area Gadramuda: a desert in western Hadapan, a former ocean drained by the 1980 earthquake; home of the borzois and their grounded artificial island, where the Deus Ex Machina and the older Xenobot are kept. About 5,090 giant Napas Fans, built by Anoir, blow breathable air into it.
- Also: Kos Angkasa ("Sky Boarding"), Mr. Rigel's food mart, Jaya Mall, Lawu City Hospital.

## Technology and food

- OAVI (pronounced "wuh-ah-vee"): chips implanted in a KS's skull, or worn for a while, that grant control over space, time, mass and gravity, powered by the brain's electrical signals. If an implanted one breaks, the KS loses every ability.
- Central OAVI: the anchor point on Earth for spacetime abilities; every spacecutting request passes through it. Destroying it would end Tobii's powers, but it is needed for Switchstar. Bambang's device is labelled "EXPERIMENT 12 OAVI SPACETIME".
- Fight Over: an Atom-∅ program on 1 January 2004 that pitted Jamal against Bambang to decide who leads Moonlocking 2. Bambang won by biting Jamal's arm with full force.
- KS Safety: a lever box labelled "KS SAFETY", ON and OFF, beside gate buttons, at Breakdown Containment. On, the KS may not harm each other; off, they may.
- Sulak: Jaya Abadi's firearm and rescue tool; fires poison darts and has a corded "duster" harpoon. "Sulak" means "duster". It is not a character.
- The Really Secret Documents (Dokumen Rahasia Banget): the folder with the tempe recipe.
- The fork bracelet: Raas's keepsake with the coordinates.
- Scabies Cream: a Jaya Abadi medicine.
- Anoir fish, by popularity: Rumham (first), Luzoir (second), Ikan Anoir (third, the shark-like fish the company and its mascot are named after), Qurai (least popular).
- A key tempe ingredient comes from the mould *Rhizopus oligosporus*. Chocolate is poison here: Erika poisons Jojo with chocolate in his food and coffee.

## The story, chapter by chapter

Spoilers throughout: the comic is complete.

- Chapter 1, Deus Ex Machina Pilot. Gix, his shoulder torn out, rests against a wall while a UFO-like craft covers a volcano, and narrates the history: The Great Blocking, Jaya Utomo, tempe, Material Limits, the partnership with Dono Diamond, Anoir and Atom-∅, the megaprojects, the fish poisoning, Xenobot-72 replacing the workers, the failed 2003 Deus Ex Machina mission and Raas Busok's disappearance.

Bambang, skull-faced, and Tobii, in his shark costume, appear; Bambang's rubber band misses Tobii and takes off Utomo's head, and the production facility explodes. Tobii opens the cages; Gigo throws Gix across the retracting bridge and falls.
- Chapter 3, You once left me with this. Bambang (face intact) and Jamal escape and pierce away their ear tattoos. Jojo and Aditya find Erika, who offers to help, and they retrieve Gigo. Exoworo grabs Gix, shows him the Really Secret Documents he "once left" with her, and lets him stay; Angkasa and Embun appear. Erika vows to kill the escaped KS and asks where Xenobot-72 is kept; Jojo shows her the older machine, and she says "...Perfect". Then the Xenobot machine captures Bambang and Jamal, its avatar flipping them off from a giant screen.
- Chapter 4, Fight Over. Two days after the explosion. Angkasa is found dead and partly eaten. Aditya orders the homeless killed; Asep and Hendra chase Gix and Exoworo, who end up at Mr. Rigel's food mart. In containment, Jamal watches the 1 January 2004 Fight Over footage while Xenobot-72 speaks to him. He says "Now... I know why", walks out of Gate 1, tears out Bambang's skull, and collapses.
- Chapter 5, Here, I'll teach you to cook. Erika finds both bodies; Tobii appears and she flips the KS Safety lever. Tobii takes Jamal and leaves him under a Sky Scraper rail, where he is crushed. Reserves run out early; people storm a soybean warehouse and the military shoots them. Jojo finds Bambang alive. Erika questions the hospitalised Gigo. Gix and Exoworo scavenge, find a soy ice cream freezer, and fry tempe; Gix reasons that Tobii's time-swapping could fetch the recipe from the past.
- Chapter 6, Anytime, whenever. Four days after the explosion. On Jaya TV, Konny says the besieged production centre is made of Material Limits. A Sky Scraper's tungsten missile kills the rioters and leaves the building standing. Jojo, who has found his own future corpse in the wreckage ("He killed me too"), rebuilds Bambang and recruits him against Tobii. Erika comes for Gix; Exoworo fights her, photographs her and swallows her whole, and Erika bursts out ("That's why you don't fight a hungry wolf"). Gix steals Tobii's food, and Tobii sends him to 2003: "If you need food so bad, take it from the past". The chapter ends on an eruption and "What year is it!!"
- Chapter 7, Came home to see the bad news. Jojo and Bambang use the Central OAVI to build a better spacecutting device. Tobii follows Gix to 2003 and finds he cannot get back without Central OAVI access for that year. Mistaken for Raas, Gix gets into Breakdown HQ; they take the recipe, flee on young Jojo's motorcycle and crash, and Gix gives the folder to 14-year-old Exoworo to keep until 2018. In the KS Testing Room Xenobot-72 welcomes them, runs the project choice and the audio test, and denies Tobii access, until Gix tells it Tobii killed Jaya Utomo and made everyone starve. Tobii denies the murder and rages at Gix; they return to 2018. Gix comes home with food and finds Exoworo apparently dead.
- Chapter 8, Don't go. Erika finds Gix and reports to Jojo ("Tell Garong I found the cat"); Gix lets Jojo take the recipe, and leaflets promise "TEMPEH WILL RETURN TOMORROW!". Erika drops a missile on Tobii's house and believes him dead; she warms to Gix and asks Gigo about his favourite food. Gix finds Exoworo's camera and her photo of Erika, confronts Erika and walks out. Tobii is alive.
- Chapter 9, The beautiful silence after the bang. Jojo is poisoned with chocolate and, sure that Tobii will come for him, waits where his future body was found. Erika vows to erupt Mount Lawu. On the factory catwalk she kills Jojo for his OAVI, which is the true cause of the factory explosion. Bambang follows; when he reaches the KS Safety, the Xenobot system cannot be found. Gix apologises to Tobii and learns that Gigo lives; the Xenobot machines are cut apart as he does. Tobii sends Erika to 2003 Mount Lawu and follows with Gix and Gigo. Bambang, who "chose swap spacetime when he first became a KS", chases Tobii through time, including through the Switchstar conference, where his rubber band kills Utomo, and under falling Sky Scrapers; he breaks Tobii's OAVI and spares him. Erika throws a superheated rock into the crater; Gigo tells her not everyone hates her ("Not everyone"). The Deus Ex Machina stops to pick up Gigo and Erika, the pause that doomed the mission. Erika tears out Gix's shoulder and leaves him, and Bambang rescues him, closing the loop with chapter 1. The closing narration says that people will not last forever, that everything ends, and that what remains is one's story in the lives of others.
- After the end: the last panel is read as an endless time loop. Creator statements relayed by the wiki: Bambang takes Gix to Lawu City Hospital in the present, where he meets the surviving Exoworo; Bambang brings Gigo and Erika back; Bambang owns Jaya Abadi and later completes Switchstar.

## Contradictions

Where the sources disagree, say so instead of picking one.

- Pronouns: the creator says Xenobot-72 is female; the wiki's prose says "they"; characters in the comic say "it" and "that robot".
- The lie: one summary says Xenobot-72 "lied to the KSs about the safety"; another says it kept the knowledge of the KS Safety from Jamal; another says it revealed the safety's use and manipulated Jamal. On the page the only charge is a character's line, "That robot lied to me / I think it lied to you too". What exactly the lie was is not on record.
- Who controls the KS Safety: Xenobot-72, by one source; a lever built into the Central OAVI, by another. In the comic Erika moves a wall lever by hand, yet the lever fails without the Xenobot-72 system. The creator also speaks of separate "KS Safety robots".
- Bambang at the lever: one source says he tries to turn the safety on, another that he tries to turn it off. The panel shows him pushing the handle toward ON.
- Clan marks: the infobox gives ⛤ and stars on both hands; the creator says the factory unit has none.
- Age and order: age 31 matches Xenobot-1 in 1987, not version 72, and when version 72 itself was made is not on record. It "replaced all workers" after the fish poisoning, yet already runs the KS intake in 2003 and Fight Over on 1 January 2004; the order of these events is not on record.
- One AI or many bodies: units 071, 072 and 074 fly together, some sources speak of "Xenobot-72s" in the plural, and an older version is also numbered 072.
- When it was destroyed: one source places it after Erika's failed missile strike in chapter 8; the explosion panel is in chapter 9.
- "Beta": the chapter 1 window says "72-BETA 3.4.0"; no source explains it.
- Global Cooling and Global Freezing are the same project; the names are used interchangeably.
- Exoworo's age in 2003: 14 by one source, 15 by another.
- KS numbers: the chapter 9 portraits show Tobii as KS-4 and Erika as KS-5; the creator called that a production error. Erika is KS-4 and Tobii is KS-5.
- General Aditya's hand mark changes from ∅ in 2003 to ⛤ in 2018, unexplained.
- Atom-∅: described as formed by Jaya Abadi joining Anoir, as Dono Diamond's company, and in chapter 1 as state-owned.
- Gix's injury: "shoulder missing" by one source, "left arm cut off" by another.
- Surviving species: fox, cat, wolf and thylacine by one source; wolves, felines, dogs and thylacines by another.
- The factory explosion: one source says Jojo was used to blow it up; another says he lost control of his OAVI while Erika killed him.
- The Sky Scraper is dated 2016 to 2018 and credited to Jamal, who was contained then; how is not explained.
- The Deus Ex Machina mission: "failed" by one source, "half-failed" by another.
